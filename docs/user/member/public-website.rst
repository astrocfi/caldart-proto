==============
Public website
==============

The public website is the part of CalDART anybody can read, without an account: the
home page, the DART directory, the news, the events, the contact page, and the
donation page. This page describes it as a visitor sees it. CalDART's website
administrator writes the pages and their words, so the titles below are the ones the
site starts with, and yours may differ.


The top menu and the footer
===========================

Every public page has the same top menu. It starts with **Home**, then the site's
sections, such as **About Us** (with **How It Works**, **History**, **DARTs**,
**Directors and Officers**, and **Sponsors** in its drop-down), **News**,
**Events**, **Join CalDART**, **Donate**, and **Contact Us**. The members-only pages
are at the right (see :doc:`members-only-content`), and last comes **Sign in**,
which opens :doc:`sign-in`. Once you are signed in, that entry says **Welcome,
<first name>** and takes you to the member portal.

The masthead shows the Duty Officer's phone number and CalDART's mailing address.
The footer repeats them, adds the contact email and the EIN, and has the **User
guide** link. That link opens this guide in a new tab once you have signed in.


The home page
=============

The home page opens with a welcome, CalDART's mission, and a row of buttons:
**Request air support**, **Join CalDART**, **Donate** when the site has a donation
page, and **Find your DART**. Below them come **Latest news**, with **All news »**,
and the missions CalDART has flown. Down the side:

* **Upcoming events**, with **All events »**.
* **Member sign-in**: *Sign in to update your profile, check your medical and
  insurance dates, or renew.* Its **Sign-in** button opens the portal.
* **Find your DART**: a **Team** list and a **Go** button, which takes you to that
  team's page. A DART, or Disaster Airlift Response Team, is a local group of
  volunteer pilots and ground crew based at one airport. The box says how many
  teams there are, and **See them all** links to the directory.
* **Membership**: each plan with its price, and a **Join CalDART** button.


The DART directory and team pages
=================================

The directory, **DARTs**, lists every team in a table: **DART**, **Airport**, and
**Leader**. Click a team to open its page. Under **This team**, it shows the team's
airport, its leader, and how to reach the leader. **« All DARTs** goes back.


News and events
===============

**News** lists posts newest first, eight to a page, with **« Newer** and
**Older »** to move through them. A members-only post only appears in the list for
readers who can open it, marked **Members only**.

**Events** lists what is **Coming up**, then what has **Already happened**, with
**« More recent** and **Older »** to page through past events twenty at a time. An
event's page says when and where it is, and **« All events** goes back. If nothing
is planned, the list says *Nothing on the calendar yet. Check back soon.*


Contact Us
==========

The contact page has CalDART's own words and, under **Reach us**, the **Duty
Officer** phone number, the contact **Email**, the **Post** address, and the
**EIN**.


Giving from the donation page
=============================

**Donate** in the top menu opens the public donation page, where anybody can give,
without an account or a password. Members and friends should give from the portal's
:doc:`donate` screen instead, so that the gift goes on their own record. The form:

#. **Amount.** Choose a gift level, or choose **Other amount** and type whole
   dollars, from $5 up to $99,999. If you don't choose an amount, it says *Choose an
   amount to give.* Under $5, it says *A donation must be at least $5.*
#. **About you.** The section opens with *Already have an account? Sign in to give
   from the portal.* **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email** (*Your receipt goes
   here*) are required. **Phone** is optional. The box takes ten digits and adds the
   dashes itself.
#. **Tell us more (optional).** This section is closed until you open it. It asks for
   your address, city, state, ZIP code, and California county; your home airport;
   your DART; your Air Care Alliance number and pilot certificate; and the volunteer
   interests you would help with.
#. **Continue to payment.** The form checks the required fields, then shows how much
   you are giving and in whose name: *You are giving $100.00 as Rosa Delgado
   (rosa@example.org).* **Change** takes you back, with everything you typed kept.
#. **Pay** from the card or PayPal tab, as on :doc:`join`.

Once the gift goes through, the form is replaced by CalDART's thanks and *A receipt
is on its way to rosa@example.org.* The receipt email has the receipt attached as a
PDF, with the sentence *No goods or services were provided in exchange for this
contribution.* If a payment fails, the form stays as it was and says why, and you
aren't charged. The card form clears so you can try another card, and each try
counts as a separate payment. After too many tries from one connection within the
hour, the page turns down more tries for a while. Wait and try again.

The page says the same thing whatever address you give. If the address already has a
portal account, the gift goes on that account, as if you had given from the portal.
But what you typed in the form isn't copied onto the account. The receipt is made out
to the name you typed and says the gift came from this page. The account's yearly
statement lists the gift separately from the account's other giving. To give with
everything on your own record, follow *Already have an account? Sign in to give from
the portal.* at the top of **About you**. It opens the sign-in screen, then the
portal's :doc:`donate` screen.

What a donor is
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Giving from this page makes you a donor: somebody who gave without joining.
CalDART keeps one record for each email address, with your name, phone, whatever
you told it in the optional section, and every gift. A donor doesn't have a password,
can't sign in, and doesn't appear in any member list. Each January, CalDART emails a
contribution statement to every donor who gave in the year before.

If you later join as a member or a friend, use the same email address in
:doc:`join` and follow the link CalDART emails there. Your record and every gift on
it become your portal account.


The page-not-found page
=======================

If you open an address that doesn't lead to a page, you see **That page is not
here**: *The address may have
changed, or the page may have been unpublished.* **Back to the home page** and
**Member portal** take you on.


If something looks wrong
========================

If a menu entry you expect is missing, or a page shows a wall instead of its words,
see :doc:`members-only-content`. If you spot a mistake in the words on a page, write
to the contact address. The website administrator looks after the pages.
