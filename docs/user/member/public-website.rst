==============
Public website
==============

The public website is the part of CalDART anybody can read, with no account: the
home page, the DART directory, the news, the events, the contact page, and the
donation page. This page describes it as a visitor sees it. The pages and their
words are written by CalDART's website administrator, so the titles below are the
ones the site starts with and yours may differ.


The top menu and the footer
===========================

Every public page has the same top menu. It starts with **Home**, then the site's
sections, such as **About Us** (with **How It Works**, **History**, **DARTs**,
**Directors and Officers**, and **Sponsors** in its drop-down), **News**,
**Events**, **Join CalDART**, **Donate**, and **Contact Us**. The members-only pages
sit at the right (see :doc:`members-only-content`), and last comes **Sign in**,
which opens :doc:`sign-in`. Once you are signed in that entry reads **Welcome,
<first name>** and takes you to the member portal.

The masthead shows the Duty Officer's phone number and CalDART's mailing address.
The footer repeats them with the contact email and the EIN, and holds the **User
guide** link, which opens this guide in a new tab once you have signed in.


The home page
=============

The home page opens with a welcome, CalDART's mission, and a row of buttons:
**Request air support**, **Join CalDART**, **Donate** when the site has a donation
page, and **Find your DART**. Below them come **Latest news**, with **All news »**,
and the missions CalDART has flown. Down the side:

* **Upcoming events**, with **All events »**.
* **Member sign-in**: *Sign in to update your profile, check your medical and
  insurance dates, or renew.* Its **Sign-in** button opens the portal.
* **Find your DART**: a **Team** list and a **Go** button, which takes you to that
  team's page. A DART, or Disaster Airlift Response Team, is a local group of
  volunteer pilots and ground crew based at one airport. The box counts the teams
  and links to **See them all**, the directory.
* **Membership**: each plan with its price, and a **Join CalDART** button.


The DART directory and team pages
=================================

The directory, **DARTs**, lists every team in a table: **DART**, **Airport**, and
**Leader**. Click a team for its page, which shows **This team** with its airport,
its leader, and how to reach the leader, and **« All DARTs** to go back.


News and events
===============

**News** lists posts newest first, eight to a page, with **« Newer** and
**Older »** to move through them. A members-only post appears in the list, marked **Members
only**, only for a reader who may open it.

**Events** lists what is **Coming up**, then what has **Already happened**, with
**« More recent** and **Older »** to page through past events twenty at a time. An event's page says when and
where it is, and **« All events** goes back. With nothing planned, the list says
*Nothing on the calendar yet. Check back soon.*


Contact Us
==========

The contact page carries CalDART's own words and, under **Reach us**, the **Duty
Officer** phone number, the contact **Email**, the **Post** address, and the
**EIN**.


Giving from the donation page
=============================

**Donate** in the top menu opens the public donation page, which takes a gift from
anybody, with no account and no password. Members and friends should give from the
portal's :doc:`donate` screen instead, so the gift lands on their own record. The
form:

#. **Amount.** Choose a tier, or **Other amount** and type whole dollars. Without an
   amount it says *Choose an amount to give.*
#. **About you.** **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** (*Your receipt goes
   here*), and **Phone** are required. The phone box takes ten digits and writes
   the dashes itself.
#. **Tell us more (optional).** Closed until you open it: your address, city, state,
   ZIP code, and California county; your home airport and its city; your DART; your
   Air Care Alliance number, pilot certificate, and whether you are IFR rated; and
   the volunteer interests you would help with.
#. **Continue to payment.** The form checks the required fields, then shows what you
   are giving and as whom: *You are giving $100.00 as Rosa Delgado
   (rosa@example.org).* **Change** takes you back with everything you typed kept.
#. **Pay** from the card or PayPal tab, as on :doc:`join`.

Once the gift goes through, the form gives way to CalDART's thanks and *A receipt
is on its way to rosa@example.org.* The receipt email has the PDF attached and the
sentence *No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution.* A
payment that fails leaves the form where it was, says why, and charges nothing.

If an account already uses the address, the page answers *An account already uses
that email address. Sign in to donate.*

What a donor is
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Giving from this page makes you a donor: somebody who gave without joining.
CalDART keeps one record for each email address, with your name, phone, whatever
you told it in the optional section, and every gift. A donor has no password, cannot
sign in, and appears in no member list. Each January a donor who gave in the year
before is emailed a contribution statement.

If you later join as a member or a friend, use the same email address in
:doc:`join` and follow the link CalDART emails there. Your record and every gift on
it become your portal account.


The page-not-found page
=======================

An address that leads nowhere shows **That page is not here**: *The address may have
changed, or the page may have been unpublished.* **Back to the home page** and
**Member portal** take you on.


If something looks wrong
========================

If a menu entry you expect is missing, or a page shows a wall in place of its words,
see :doc:`members-only-content`. For a mistake in the words on a page, write to the
contact address; the website administrator looks after the pages.
