=========
Dashboard
=========

The **Dashboard** is the portal's home page, where you land after signing in. It
greets you with **Welcome, <first name>** and answers three questions, in order: is
my membership current, what can I read, and what have I paid?

You reach it once you have finished joining. That means your address is verified
and your profile is complete. If you joined as a member, it also means you made your
first payment or chose to be a friend instead. Until then, every portal screen, this
one included, opens the next step of :doc:`join`. If the office made your account for
you, you reach the dashboard as soon as your address is verified, and you pay your
dues from here.


What you see
============

The cards run down the page in this order. Each one appears only when it applies to
you.

If you are a DART leader and your profile doesn't name a DART, a box above the cards
says *Bulk email needs a DART. Set yours on "My profile".* Its **Open "My profile"**
button takes you to where you set the DART (see :doc:`../bulk-email/dart-leaders`).

**Membership**
  A colored dot and a word show your membership's state, with the date it runs to or
  ran out on. The five states are:

  * **Current**: a term covers today.
  * **Expiring soon**: current, with 30 days or fewer to run.
  * **Expired**: your last term has run out.
  * **Not yet paid**: you joined as a member and haven't paid your dues.
  * **Friend**: you are a friend of CalDART, so nothing expires.

  The card's heading says the same in words, such as **Your membership is
  current** or **Your membership has expired**. From 30 days before expiry onward,
  the card gets a colored edge and **Renew** becomes its main button. After expiry
  the button says **Renew now**. **Update your details** opens :doc:`profile`, which
  is also where you can choose to become a friend instead. If you have already asked
  to become a friend, the card says so, as in *You become a friend on 03/02/2027.*,
  with an **Undo** button.

  A life member's card is headed **Lifetime member** and shows the **Never expires**
  state. It says *Nothing to renew*, with thanks for joining for life. It doesn't
  have a button.

  A friend's card is labeled **Friend of CalDART** and headed **You are a friend of
  CalDART**. It says *No dues and no expiry. Become a member any time.* and has a
  **Make me a member** button (see :doc:`become-a-member`). It never gets the colored
  edge.

  If the office made your account and you haven't paid yet, the card is headed **Your
  membership is not yet paid**. It says *Pay your dues to become a member of
  CalDART.*, and its **Pay dues** button opens the checkout (see
  :doc:`become-a-member`).

**Member content**
  The members-only pages you can read, taken straight from the public site (see
  :doc:`members-only-content`). If your membership has lapsed, the card says
  *Members-only pages are open to current members*, with a **Renew to read them
  again** button that opens :doc:`renew`. If there aren't any members-only pages yet,
  it says **Nothing published yet**. A friend, or a member who hasn't paid yet,
  doesn't see this card, unless a staff role lets them read those pages anyway.

**Recent payments**
  First, one line says whether automatic renewal is on, and if it is, how much the
  next charge is and when: *Automatic renewal is on: $145.00 will be charged on
  04/27/2027.* For a life member, the line is about their recurring donation. A friend
  doesn't have anything to renew, so a friend only sees the line if they have a
  recurring donation. Below it are your last five payments. Each shows the **Date** it
  was paid, what it was **For** (in the same words as :doc:`payments`, such as
  **Annual**, **Annual and contribution**, or **Donation**), the **Amount**, and the
  **Status** as a colored dot and a word. **All payments, receipts, and renewals**
  opens :doc:`payments`. If you don't have anything to renew (a friend, a life member,
  or a member who hasn't paid yet), the link says **All payments and receipts**.

**Quick links**
  A few next steps. The menu has everything else. Everybody gets **Renew** (or
  **Donate**, for a friend or a life member), **My profile**, **My aircraft**, and
  **Email to me**. A DART leader, a verifier, or an administrator also gets **Member
  check**. A treasurer or an account administrator gets **Accounting**, and an account
  administrator gets **Members**. The list stops at five links. Your role's own
  screens come first, so **Email to me**, and then **My aircraft**, drop off to make
  room.


Finding your way around
=======================

The menu down the left of every portal screen has these groups: **My account**,
**Operations**, **Bulk email**, **Finance**, **Administration**, and **System**. It only
shows the screens your roles open. The small heading above each screen's title names
its group.

Every signed-in person has **My account**: **Dashboard**, **My profile**, **My
aircraft**, **My payments**, **Donate**, **Renew**, **Change password**, **Change
email**, **Email to me** (:doc:`messages`), and **Email preferences**
(:doc:`email-preferences`). A friend and a life member don't have a **Renew** entry,
because neither has a term to renew. A life member gives through **Donate**.

The other groups belong to DART leaders, CalDART management, and administrators (see
:doc:`../roles`). When the menu is longer than the window, it scrolls separately from
the page. A shadow at its bottom shows that there are more entries below. It always
opens with your current screen in view.

Every date and time in the portal is Pacific time, written like *10/04/2026 at 5:33
AM*.

At the bottom of the menu, **User guide** opens this guide in a new tab, at its
beginning, and **Back to caldart.org** returns you to the public site.

The top bar has **Help**, which opens this guide's page for your current screen in a
new tab, then your name and **Sign out**. On a phone or a narrow window, the menu
folds away behind the **Menu** button in the top bar. That button opens the menu at
the top of the page, and Escape closes it again. Every screen works from the
keyboard. When you press Tab, *Skip to content* comes first.


When a screen won't open
========================

**Not allowed**
  *You do not have access to this page.* The screen is for a role you don't hold,
  and the message names that role, for example *It is open to the Account
  administrator or Treasurer role.* You haven't done anything wrong. Ask a CalDART
  administrator if you should have the role. **Go to the dashboard** takes you
  home.

**Page not found**
  *That page is not part of the member portal.* The address is mistyped or out of
  date. **Go to the dashboard** takes you home. If you aren't signed in, **Go to the
  CalDART home page** takes you to the public site instead.

**This page did not load**
  The screen didn't finish downloading, usually because the connection dropped or
  the site was updated while you had the page open. Press **Reload** to try again.


If something looks wrong
========================

If you paid and the card still says **Expired**, **Not yet paid**, or **Friend**,
reload the page first. Then look at **Recent payments**. A payment marked **Failed**
didn't take any money, so try again from :doc:`renew`. If a payment is marked
**Paid** but your membership doesn't show it, something has gone wrong. Tell the
office the date and the amount. If a menu entry you expect is missing, you don't
have the role for it. A user administrator can grant it. For a role that needs
two-factor sign-in, a system administrator can grant it, or a user administrator who
holds that role (see :doc:`../roles`).
