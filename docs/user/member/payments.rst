===========
My payments
===========

**My payments** covers everything about your money: whether CalDART renews your
membership for you, whether you give on a schedule, everything you have paid with a
receipt for each, and a contribution statement for each year you gave. Open it from
**My payments** in the menu, or from **All payments, receipts, and renewals** on your
:doc:`dashboard`. Under the title **My payments**, the screen says *Your automatic
renewal, recurring donation, payments and receipts, and tax statements.* For a friend
or a life member, that line leaves out the automatic renewal.


What you see
============

The cards run down the page in this order.

**Automatic renewal**
  Whether CalDART will renew your membership for you, and the buttons that change
  that. A life member and a friend don't have anything to renew, so they don't see
  this card. The exception is a renewal that is still switched on. Then the card
  appears, so that they can turn it off.

**Recurring donation**
  Whether you give on a schedule. Its status is **On**, **Off**, or **Stopped**. It
  shows the **Method**, **Amount**, **How often**, and **Next charge**, with
  **Change** and **Turn off** buttons. If you haven't set one up, the card says *A
  recurring donation charges a saved card or PayPal account monthly, quarterly, or
  yearly, for the amount you choose.*, with a **Set up** button that opens
  :doc:`donate`. That page describes setting up and changing a recurring donation.

**Your payments**
  Everything you have paid CalDART, newest first: **Date**, **For** (the plan, the plan *and
  contribution*, or **Donation** for a gift on its own), **Amount**, **Refunded** (only if
  you have had money back), **Status**, and **Receipt**. The status is **Paid**,
  **Pending**, **Failed**, **Partly refunded**, or **Refunded**. The **Receipt** link
  downloads the same PDF that came with the receipt email. A failed or pending
  payment doesn't have a receipt, because the money didn't arrive. On a phone, the
  **For** column and then **Refunded** are hidden when the table doesn't fit. The
  **Date** stays fixed at the left, and you scroll the table sideways to see the
  **Amount**, the **Status**, and the **Receipt**.

**Contribution statements**
  One button for each calendar year you gave in, such as **2026 statement**.


Automatic renewal
=================

Automatic renewal renews an Annual membership each year from a saved card or PayPal
account, so it never lapses because a reminder arrived in a busy week. It is always
yearly.

Turning it on
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Check **Renew automatically each year** when you pay for a membership, or press
**Turn on** on the card here. **Turn on** saves a payment method without charging
it. It opens the same choices as the checkout: the plan that will renew, the
contribution to renew with it, **First charge on**, and a tab for each payment
method. The first plan on the list that renews is chosen for you. If the site
doesn't have a membership plan set up, it shows *No membership plan is set up yet.
Ask an administrator.* instead, and you can't save a method. The line above the tabs
says what will happen, for example *CalDART will charge $145.00 on 03/01/2027, and
each year after that. We will email you 14 days before every charge.*

You choose the **First charge on** date. It starts at the day your membership runs
out, which is the day most people want, or at today if your membership isn't
current. If you pick an earlier day, you renew before your term has run out, so you
pay for months you already had. If you pick a later day, your membership lapses from
the day it runs out until the charge. A day in the past gets *The next charge cannot
be in the past.*

Once it is on, CalDART emails you *CalDART: automatic renewal is on*, with the
amount, the card, and the day of the next charge.

What the card shows
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

While it is on, the status is **On**, and the card shows the **Method**, the
**Plan**, the **Yearly contribution** it takes with the dues, and the **Next charge**
with its amount. If the charge comes after your membership runs out, the date is
followed by *after your membership runs out on* and that date. While automatic
renewal is on, you don't get the usual renewal reminders (see :doc:`renew`).

**Change** opens **Change your renewal**: the plan, the contribution, and **Next
charge on**. If only one plan renews, the form just names it. Press **Save
changes**. You pay whatever the chosen plan costs on the day of the charge. A charge
already scheduled keeps its own day. So if you set a day while a charge is waiting,
your day applies to the charge after it. After each charge, the next charge day
moves to the end of the term that charge paid for.

**Turn off** asks *Turn automatic renewal off?* in a panel under the card's buttons.
**Turn it off** stops automatic renewal at once. You aren't charged again, CalDART
forgets the saved payment method, and your membership still runs to the end of the
term you paid for. **Cancel**, or Escape, doesn't change anything. CalDART emails
you *CalDART: automatic renewal is off*, and the usual reminders start again.

The emails it sends
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* **Fourteen days before every charge**: *CalDART: we will renew your membership
  on* the day of the charge (or *…renew your membership and take your
  contribution on…*), with the amount, the date, and the card that will be used.
* **When the card expires before the next charge**: *CalDART: the card we renew
  your membership with expires soon*, in time to save another.
* **When the charge goes through**: *CalDART: your membership has been renewed*,
  with your new expiry date and the receipt PDF attached. That email is your
  receipt, and a separate one doesn't follow.
* **When a charge is refused**: *CalDART: we could not renew your membership*, with
  the reason the bank gave.
* **When the treasurer closes a charge that couldn't be completed**: the same
  subject. Automatic renewal is paused, so you won't be charged automatically. The
  email says when your membership ends (or that it has ended). It links to the
  **Renew** screen so you can renew by hand, and mentions **Turn on again** on this
  screen. The treasurer may also get in touch with you.

If a charge is refused
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

CalDART tries again the next day, then three days after that, then a week after
that. If all four tries are refused, automatic renewal turns itself off, CalDART
tells you so, and the usual reminders take over. Your current membership still runs
to its end date. The card's status then says **Stopped**, with the reason the last
charge was refused. **Turn on again** saves another method and starts automatic
renewal again. If the treasurer closed the last charge, the card says *Automatic
renewal is paused because the last charge couldn't be completed, so you won't be
charged automatically.* instead of the reason.

CalDART makes renewal charges overnight. If it misses your charge day, for instance
because that night's run failed, the next run makes the charge straight away, and
the email says so. The new term starts on the day the money arrives. If your
membership has been lapsed for more than a month, CalDART doesn't charge you without
asking. Instead, automatic renewal turns itself off, the email says why, and you
renew by hand.


Receipts
========

CalDART emails a receipt the moment a payment clears, whether you paid by card,
through PayPal, or by a check somebody at CalDART entered for you. The subject is
*CalDART: your receipt for $45.00*, with your own amount, and the receipt is attached
as a PDF. It shows CalDART's name, address, and EIN, a receipt number, the date,
what you paid for, and the total. A receipt for a contribution also includes the
sentence *No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution.*
Dues are listed as dues, because a membership is something you received.

You can download any receipt again from the **Receipt** link on its row.


Refunds
=======

There isn't a refund button for members. Ask the office. A treasurer or an account
administrator can refund all or part of a payment. CalDART emails you *CalDART: a
refund of $45.00*, with the payment it came from, the reason, and whether your
membership ended with it. The money goes back to the card or account you paid with,
usually within a few business days.


Contribution statements
=======================

A statement puts a calendar year of contributions on one page: the date, receipt
number, and amount of each, anything refunded, and the year's total, with the
wording your tax preparer needs. It is the paper you file with a tax return.

Each January, CalDART emails a statement to every member, friend, and donor who gave
in the year before. The subject is *CalDART: your 2026 contribution statement*, and
the statement is attached as a PDF. The buttons here download the same statement at
any time.

If you paid dues in a year but didn't give anything, that year doesn't have a
statement, because dues aren't a gift. If you don't have any statements, the card
says **No statements yet**. A refund comes off the contribution first, even when the
payment also covered dues. So if you had a refund in a year, check that year's
statement against your receipts before you file it.


If something looks wrong
========================

If a card says *We couldn't load your automatic renewal settings.* (or *recurring
donation settings*), CalDART can't show you whether it will
charge you. Press **Try again**, and contact CalDART if it keeps happening. To
change the card on file, turn automatic renewal or the recurring donation off and
set it up again with the new card. CalDART only keeps a card on file for these two.
Any other payment asks for your card details again.
