============
Join CalDART
============

The **Join CalDART** wizard creates your account and, for a member, takes your
dues. *Joining takes about three minutes.* Membership is yearly or for life,
and the pay step shows the prices. You can also join as a friend of CalDART, a
supporter who doesn't pay dues.

You open it from **Join CalDART** in the public site's top menu, from the **Join
CalDART** buttons on the home page, or from *Not a member yet?* on the sign-in
screen.


How the wizard works
====================

The wizard has five steps, shown in a row at the top: **Account**, **Verify**,
**Profile**, **Pay**, and **Done**. Each one is checked off when you finish it. Each
step's card is as wide as the title and the row of steps above it. When you finish a
step, the cursor moves to the next step's heading.

Until you finish, the wizard is all of the portal you can use. There isn't a menu
down the left, and any other portal screen you open brings you back to your current
step. The header still shows **Help**, your address, and **Sign out**, and you can
still open this guide from **Help**.

You can close the tab or sign out at any point and come back later, on any browser
or device. When you sign in again, the wizard puts you back on the step you last
saw. It works out how far you got from your address, your profile, and your
membership. You leave a step only by finishing it. You can always go back to an
earlier step, but you can't skip ahead past one you haven't finished.

You have finished joining once your address is verified and your profile is
complete. If you chose **Join as a member**, your first payment must also clear,
unless you choose on the pay step to be a friend instead. The pay step only holds a
member who has never had a paid membership. A member whose membership has expired
has already joined. They keep the portal and renew from it (see :doc:`renew`).

A friend has finished once they leave the pay step, either by giving or by pressing
**Continue without a gift**. Giving is optional. Until then, a friend joining for the
first time stays in the wizard, and reloading the pay step keeps them on it. If a
friend closes the tab at the pay step and signs in again later, they have finished,
because a friend doesn't owe anything.

If an administrator created your account, you have already joined. Until you verify
the address, signing in shows only **Verify your email address** and the **Check
your email** card. Once the address is verified, your first sign-in opens the
dashboard. There isn't a profile or pay step, and you fill in the rest of your
profile when you like.

Once you have joined, the **Profile** and **Pay** steps send you to your
:doc:`dashboard`, so going back into the wizard can't charge you again or change
your kind of account.


Step 1: Account
===============

The step is headed **Create your account** and opens with the question **How would
you like to join?** On a computer the two choices are side by side. The one you
choose is shaded, with a dark edge.

**Join as a member**
  *Pay yearly or lifetime dues and you are a member right away.* The page opens with
  this one chosen.

**Join as a friend**
  *No dues. Support CalDART when you like, and become a member any time.*

Then give your **First name**, **Last name**, **Email address**, and a
**Password**. You will sign in with the email address, so use one you check. The
password needs at least 8 characters and can't be a common one such as password1
(the rules are on :doc:`reset-password`). Press **Create account**. If any box is
empty or mistyped, the form marks all of them at once, and the line beside the
button says how many to check.

If you already have an account, as a member or a friend, follow **Already have an
account? Sign in** and the wizard picks up where you left off. If you are already
signed in, the step says **Your account** with your address. It has two buttons:
**Continue**, and **Use a different account**, which signs you out.

Once every box is right, the step changes to **Check your email**: *We sent a message
to marta.reyes@example.org. Follow the link in it to continue.* You aren't signed in
yet. The screen says the same thing whatever the address, so nobody can use it to
find out who has an account. The email tells you what to do next:

* **A new address** gets *CalDART: verify your email address*. Its link opens
  :doc:`verify-email`. Press **Continue**, sign in with your address and password,
  and the wizard goes on to your profile.
* **An address you used before as a donor** gets a message asking you to open a
  portal account for it. Your past gifts stay with the address. Open the link, choose
  the password you will sign in with, press **Open my account**, then **Continue**,
  and sign in with that password.
* **An address that already has an account** gets *CalDART: you already have an
  account*, with links to sign in and to choose a new password. The account itself
  doesn't change. If it is deactivated, the email says to sign in and press
  **Reactivate my account** (see :doc:`sign-in`). If it is closed, the email says to
  contact CalDART.

No message? Look in your spam folder, then sign in to send it again.


Step 2: Verify
==============

You see this step if you sign in before you follow the link in *CalDART: verify your
email address*. It waits until you open the link. Press **Continue** on the **Email
verified** screen (see :doc:`verify-email`), and the wizard moves on.

* **I've clicked the link** checks again. Use it if you opened the link in another
  browser or on your phone. If the address isn't verified yet, the step says *Not
  verified yet. Open the link in the message we sent, then try again.*
* **Resend verification message** sends another. Look in your spam folder first.
* **Use a different email address** opens :doc:`change-email`, where you can fix a
  mistyped address. It then brings you back here and shows the address the link went
  to. Following that link moves the account to the address and verifies it.

You can't use anything else in the portal until you verify your address. If you sign
in again before you do, you land back here. The link works for three days. If you
verified your address before, the wizard skips this step.


Step 3: Profile
===============

The step is headed **About you**: *CalDART needs a way to reach you during an
activation.* It is the same form you later keep up to date on :doc:`profile`, which
describes every field. Your name is left off, because you gave it on the first step.
Each phone number shares a line with its extension. When the state is California,
**California county** starts at *Choose a county*. For any other state it says *Not
in California*. Only **Address**, **City**, **State**, and **ZIP code** are required
at this step, and every phone number is optional. **Pilot certificate** always has a
value. Leave it at *Not a pilot* if you aren't one. Everything else can wait.

As you type your street into **Address**, matching addresses appear in a list
under the box. Pick yours, and the form fills in **City**, **State**, **ZIP code**,
and **California county** for you. :doc:`profile` describes the list in full.

Press **Save and continue**. The form saves only when every field it checks is
right. Once it saves, you move on.


Step 4: Pay
===========

The step starts from the choice you made on the account step. It offers three
options: pay your dues as a member, give a donation as a friend, or go on as a friend
without giving. You can change your mind on this step as often as you like.

A member's step is headed **Pay your dues**. Membership starts the moment the
payment clears, so you don't wait for anybody. To get past this step, you either pay
or choose to be a friend instead.

#. **Membership.** Choose **Annual** ($45.00, *One year*) or **Life** ($650.00,
   *One payment, membership for life*). Annual, the first on the list, is chosen
   for you. If a site offers only one plan, the step just names it. If the step says
   *No membership plan is set up yet. Ask an administrator.*, CalDART hasn't set up
   its plans on this site, so ask the office. Under the plans, **Join as a friend
   instead (no dues)** turns the step into a friend's **Donate to CalDART**,
   described below.
#. **Add a contribution.** CalDART is a 501(c)(3), so a contribution on top of your
   dues is tax deductible and pays for training, fuel, and equipment. Choose a tier
   (Participating $20, Bronze $100, Silver $300, Gold $1,000, Diamond $3,000, or
   Platinum $10,000), choose **Other amount** and type your own amount in whole
   dollars, or choose **No thank you**. **Total today** adds it up as you go.
#. **Renew automatically each year.** Check it if you want CalDART to renew the
   membership for you each year with the card or PayPal account you pay with. *We
   will email you 14 days before each charge to your card or PayPal account, and
   you can turn it off at any time from Payments.* See :doc:`payments` for more.
#. **How would you like to pay?** Choose a tab and pay. The tabs are described
   below.

A friend's step is headed **Donate to CalDART** instead: *Friends don't pay dues. A
donation of any size helps.* It offers only a donation, and it starts without an
amount chosen. You pay the same ways, and you can choose **Make this a recurring
donation** (see :doc:`donate`). Until you choose an amount, **Continue without a
gift** is the main button. Press it to finish as a friend without paying anything.
Once you choose an amount, that button stays at the bottom of the card, looking plainer.

Under the donation amounts, **I changed my mind, I want to be a member** turns the
step into **Pay your dues**. There, **Join as a friend instead (no dues)** turns it
back. Your account changes only when you leave the step. Paying dues makes you a
member. Leaving a friend's step, with or without a donation, makes you a friend. If
you reload the page, the step shows the kind of account CalDART has on record for
you. You can also become a member any time later (see :doc:`become-a-member`).

The payment tabs
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Card · Apple Pay · Google Pay**
  Type a card number, or tap the **Apple Pay** or **Google Pay** button above the
  card fields when your browser and device offer it. Then press the button that says
  **Pay** and the amount. Your card details go straight to Stripe, the payment
  service for cards, and CalDART never sees them. With some cards, your bank asks you to
  confirm the payment on its own page. You then come back to **Finishing your
  payment** while the wizard checks with Stripe.

**PayPal**
  Opens a PayPal window. Sign in, approve, and you come straight back. If you close
  the window without paying, the note says *Payment canceled* and you aren't
  charged.

**Test payment**
  Appears only on a practice copy of the site, which doesn't take real money. Its
  **Succeed** and **Fail** buttons pretend to pay.

You only see the tabs for the ways this site accepts. If none is set up, the step says
**Online payment is not set up yet** and asks you to contact CalDART to pay by
check.


Step 5: Done
============

The line under the title says *You are a member of CalDART.*, or *You are a friend of
CalDART.* for a friend. The step is headed **Welcome to CalDART**, with your
membership status and the date your membership runs until. A life member sees *You
are a life member. You never have to pay anything more.* A friend sees *You are a
friend of CalDART, without dues or an expiry date. Become a member any time.*

If you paid on the step before, it also says that a receipt is on its way to your
inbox. The email has the subject *CalDART: your receipt for $45.00* (with your own
amount), and the receipt is attached as a PDF. You can download it again from
:doc:`payments`.

**Go to my dashboard** takes you on. Once your membership is current, there is also
**Add the planes I fly** (see :doc:`my-aircraft`). From here the whole portal is
open, with its menu down the left. A member also sees **What you can read now**, a
list of the members-only pages the membership opens (see :doc:`members-only-content`).

If the step is headed **Almost there**, your payment hasn't cleared yet, and you are
a friend until it does. CalDART emails you when it clears, and you are a member from
that moment.


If something looks wrong
========================

*Your payment is still being processed.* means your bank or wallet is a few seconds
behind your browser. It is safe to close the page. CalDART emails you when the
payment clears, and your membership starts by itself.

If your card is declined, you aren't charged. Try another card or the PayPal tab. If
you were charged but the wizard still asks you to pay, go back to the wizard from the
**Join CalDART** menu entry. If that doesn't fix it, contact the office with the date
and the amount. CalDART never counts a payment twice, so asking is safe.
