===========
Email to me
===========

On **Email to me**, you can read again the bulk emails CalDART has sent you. Bulk email is the
news CalDART writes to many members and friends at once, such as meeting announcements,
appeals for donations, and requests for pilots. If you deleted one, joined after it went
out, or your mail program shows it badly, you can read it here. Open it from **My account**
in the menu. The line under the title says *Copies of emails sent to me by CalDART.*


What you see
============

One line for each bulk email CalDART sent you, the newest first:

- **Date**: the day it was sent to you.
- **Subject**: the subject line of your copy. Click it to open the email, or, for a
  mission callout, the page where you answer it (:doc:`callouts`).
- **From**: who at CalDART sent it.
- **Type**: the type of email, such as *Operational*.

The arrow on **Date** shows the order. Click a heading to sort by it. On a narrow screen,
**Type** and then **From** are hidden. On a phone, the table scrolls sideways, says so above
it, and keeps **Subject** fixed at the left. Until CalDART has sent you a bulk email, the
list says *No messages yet*, with an **Email preferences** button that opens
:doc:`email-preferences`, where you choose which kinds of email you get.

The list only shows bulk emails sent to you, never anybody else's. Email about your own
account, such as receipts, renewal reminders, and password links, isn't bulk email, so it
isn't listed here. If the mail server refused your copy when CalDART sent it, for example
because your address was wrong at the time, that email isn't listed either. If an email went
out but your own mail server sent it back as undeliverable, it is listed, so you can read it
here.


Reading an email
================

Click the subject to open the email on its own page. The page shows the subject as its
heading, a line saying who sent it, on which day, and what type of email it is, and then the
email itself, exactly as you received it. If the email used your details, such as your first
name or your DART, it shows them as they were when it was sent, even if you have changed
them since.

**See all your messages** takes you back to the list.

Pictures in the email show as they do in your mail program, and its links open in a new tab,
so the email stays open.


View this email in your browser
===============================

Every bulk email ends with a link, *View this email in your browser*, just above the lines
about why you get it. It opens the same page for that email on this screen. If you aren't
signed in, the site asks you to sign in first, then shows the email. In a mission callout,
the link opens the page where you answer it instead (:doc:`callouts`).


If something looks wrong
========================

If an email you remember is missing, CalDART may have taken it off this list since it was
sent, for example a call for volunteers that doesn't apply any more. Its page then says
*This message is not available.* You see the same message if somebody passes you a link to
an email that was sent to them. To stop getting a type of bulk email, use
:doc:`email-preferences`.
