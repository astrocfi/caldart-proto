==========
My profile
==========

**My profile** holds the details CalDART uses to reach you during an activation
and to check that you are current to fly. It also shows whether you are a member
or a friend and lets you switch. You can also set up two-factor sign-in and
deactivate your account here.


What you see
============

The form is the one you filled in when you joined, with your first and last name
added at the top. It shows what CalDART has on file. The **My aircraft** link at the
top right opens :doc:`my-aircraft`. Below the form are three cards: **Your kind of
account**, **Two-factor sign-in**, and **Deactivate my account**. The **Two-factor
sign-in** card says whether you need a code from your phone to sign in, and how many
backup codes you have left. It has the **Turn on**, **New backup codes**, and **Turn
off** buttons (see :doc:`two-factor`).

Contact
~~~~~~~

**First name**, **Last name**, **Phone**, **Alternate phone**, **Address**, **Address line 2**, **City**,
**State**, **ZIP code**, **California county**, **Emergency contact**, and
**Emergency contact phone**.

* **First name** and **Last name** are the name on your account, the one CalDART
  greets you by and lists you under. Both are required.
* Every phone number is optional, your own included: CalDART reaches you by email.
* If you type a name all in capitals or all in lower case, it is saved in title
  case: ``SMITH`` is saved as **Smith**, ``mcdonald`` as **McDonald**, ``o'brien``
  as **O'Brien** (with a curly apostrophe too), ``a.j.`` as **A.J.**, and
  ``smith-jones`` as **Smith-Jones**. A part without a vowel is treated as initials
  and saved in capitals, so ``tj`` and ``TJ`` are both saved as **TJ**. After the
  first word, *van*, *von*, *der*, *den*, *de*, *del*, *della*, *da*, *di*, *du*,
  *la*, and *le* stay lower case, so ``JAN VAN DER BERG`` is saved as **Jan van der
  Berg**, and *II*, *III*, and *IV* stay capitals. In **Last name**, they also stay
  lower case as the first word if another word follows, so ``van dyke`` is saved as
  **van Dyke**. If you type a name with both capitals and lower case, such as
  **DeAnna**, **MacArthur**, or **van Dyke**, it is saved exactly as you typed it.
  So if the rule gets your name wrong, type it that way. Either way, spaces at the
  start and end, and extra spaces between words, are dropped.
* **Emergency contact** is a person's name too, and is saved by the same rule.
* Each phone number has its own **ext.** box beside it for an extension.
* A phone box takes only digits and adds the dashes for you as you type, so a
  number looks like 415-555-0100. It won't take an eleventh digit. If you type a
  letter in the middle of a number, the number and the cursor stay as they were. **Phone** and
  **Alternate phone** both say *10 digits, such as 415-555-0100* under the label.
* **State** and **California county** are lists. The county list starts with
  *Not in California*.
* **ZIP code** takes five digits.
* **Address** suggests addresses as you type, and says so under the label: *Start
  typing the whole address, city included, then pick it from the list.* Once you
  type three characters and pause, a list of matching US addresses opens under the
  box, with California addresses first. Click one, or move to it with the up and down
  arrow keys and press Enter. It fills in **Address**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP
  code**, and **California county** all at once. It leaves **Address line 2** alone,
  so add an apartment or suite there. Press Escape, or click anywhere else, to close
  the list and keep what you typed. You can still change any field after you pick an
  address. If a list doesn't appear, type the full address as usual.
* **Address**, **Address line 2**, and **City** are saved in title case, every word,
  however you type them: ``123 main st`` is saved as **123 Main St** and ``palo
  alto`` as **Palo Alto**. A word with a digit in it, such as a unit number, stays
  exactly as you typed it. After an apostrophe, a name keeps its capital and a
  possessive stays lower case: **O'Brien Ln** and **King's Rd**.

Amateur radio
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Amateur radio callsign**, under the emergency contact. It is optional. It takes a
US callsign, such as W6ABC or KD6AB, and capitalizes it as you type.

Aviation
~~~~~~~~

**Home airport** (three characters, with the leading K left off: PAO, E16),
**Secondary airport** (another field you fly from, with the same three-character
rule), **DART** (your main Disaster Airlift Response Team, a local group of
volunteers at one airport; *Not decided yet* if you haven't chosen), **Air Care
Alliance number**, **Pilot certificate**, **Certificate number**, **Medical**,
**Medical expires**, **Photo ID**, **Last flight review**, and **Total hours**.

* **Home airport** and **Secondary airport** each take three letters or digits,
  and turn them into capitals as you type. If you type the four-letter form, such as
  KSQL, the K drops off when you type the fourth letter, leaving SQL. You can leave
  either one blank.

* **Pilot certificate** offers *Not a pilot*, Student, Sport, Recreational,
  Private, Commercial, and Airline transport pilot.
* **Certificate number** takes only the seven digits of your pilot certificate, and
  says *7 digits* under the label. **Certificate number** and **Medical expires** are
  both optional, even after you choose a certificate or a medical.
* **Medical** offers *None*, BasicMed, First class, Second class, and Third class.
* **Photo ID** offers *Not provided*, Driver's license, Passport, State ID card,
  Military ID, and Other. CalDART only records the kind of document, never its
  number or its dates.
* Under **Pilot certificate**, **Medical expires**, and **Photo ID**, each item shows
  a mark. **Verified** says who verified it and on which day. **Not yet verified**, in
  amber, is the usual state until you show the documents. If you chose **Not a
  pilot**, a medical of **None**, or a photo ID of **Not provided**, there isn't
  anything to verify, so that item doesn't show a mark. The medical's mark sits with
  its expiration date, because that is the date a verifier checks against the
  certificate. Once the date has passed, the mark says **Expired** first, verified or
  not. Under the first item is *A DART leader or verifier checks these against the
  documents.* You can't verify them yourself here. Show the documents to a DART
  leader or a verifier.
* **Aircraft**: check **I fly rented or borrowed aircraft** if you don't own an
  aircraft to list, so that a DART leader knows why your aircraft list is empty.
* **Ratings**: ASEL, AMEL, ASES, AMES, Helicopter, and Instrument on one row; CFI,
  CFII, and MEI on the next. Check **Instrument** if you hold an instrument rating.
  This is the only place the profile records it.

Saving your changes
===================

Change anything and press **Save changes**. Once you change a field, the **Save
changes** row stays at the bottom of the window as you scroll, so you can save from
anywhere on the form. When you save, *Profile saved.* appears at the bottom of the
screen, and the cursor stays on **Save changes**. Saving replaces the whole profile,
so a field you empty is saved empty. Your aircraft are on their own screen, and
saving the profile doesn't touch them.

The form checks each field as you leave it. If a field is wrong, it turns red with
the reason under it, and the form won't save until you fix it. If you press **Save
changes** while a field is still wrong, the cursor goes to the first wrong field,
and a line beside the button says how many fields to check:

* *Enter your first name.* and *Enter your last name.* when either
  name is blank.
* *Enter your street address.*, *Enter your city.*, *Choose your state.*, and
  *Enter your ZIP code.* for the details every profile needs.
* *Use a ten-digit number like 415-555-0100.* when a phone number is too short.
* *Use a 5-digit ZIP code, such as 95035.* CalDART doesn't collect ZIP+4.
* *Use a three-character identifier like PAO, E16, or KLS.* Typing the four-letter
  form drops the K, so KPAO becomes PAO. A three-character identifier that starts
  with K keeps it, so KLS stays KLS.
* *Enter the 7 digits of the pilot certificate number.* for a certificate number
  shorter than seven digits.
* *Enter your total hours as a whole number.*
* *Enter a US amateur radio callsign, such as W6ABC.* for a callsign from outside
  the US.

Keep your **Medical expires** date, your certificate, and your phone current. A
DART leader checks them before a mission, and CalDART treats a medical as current
only up to the date you entered.

If you change your pilot certificate or its number, your medical or its date, or
your photo ID, that item loses its verification when you save. Its mark says **Not
yet verified** until a DART leader or a verifier checks the new details. Until all
three are verified again, a DART leader sees you as a NO-GO.


Your kind of account
====================

The card says which you are: *You are a member of CalDART.*, *You are a life member
of CalDART.*, *Your membership is not yet paid. Pay your dues to become a member of
CalDART.* (with a **Pay dues** button) if the office made your account and you
haven't paid yet, or *You are a friend of CalDART, without dues or an expiry date.
Become a member any time.* A friend of CalDART has the same portal as a member but
doesn't pay dues. So nothing expires, CalDART doesn't send renewal reminders, and
members-only pages stay closed. **Make me a friend** is only on this card. Your
:doc:`dashboard` also shows a change that is waiting, with its **Undo**, and a
friend's **Make me a member**. A life member stays a member, and sees neither button.

**Make me a friend**
  Opens a short confirmation below the card's buttons. If your membership is current,
  it says *Your membership stays current through 03/01/2027. On 03/02/2027 you become
  a friend of CalDART, without dues, an expiry date, or renewal reminders.* You keep
  what you paid for. If your membership isn't current, you become a friend today.

  Either way, automatic renewal is turned off, and CalDART emails you to say so
  (subject *CalDART: automatic renewal is off*). If your renewal also gives a
  contribution each year, the confirmation asks *Your automatic renewal also gives
  $100 each year. Keep giving $100 a year as a recurring donation?* **Become a friend
  and keep giving $100 a year** keeps the gift going as a yearly recurring donation,
  charged to the same card. The first charge is on the day your renewal would have
  been. **Become a friend and stop the $100** ends the gift along with the renewal.
  Both buttons show your own amount. If you already have a recurring donation, you
  can't keep the gift this way, and the screen asks you to change that donation on
  :doc:`donate`. Otherwise the confirmation has one **Yes, make me a friend** button.
  **Cancel**, or Escape, doesn't change anything.

**Undo**
  Until the day of the change, the card says *You become a friend on 03/02/2027.*,
  with an **Undo** button. **Undo** keeps you a member. It doesn't turn automatic
  renewal back on. Do that on :doc:`payments`, or renew. If you renew before the day
  of the change, you also stay a member.

**Make me a member**
  A friend's button. It opens :doc:`become-a-member`.


Deactivating your account
=========================

The last card, **Deactivate my account**, says *You will be signed out and will not
appear in any list. Your information and your payment history are kept. Sign in
again any time to reactivate.* If your membership is current, it also says that
deactivating ends the membership straight away, and that it starts again if you
reactivate before its end date. For a life member, it says that the lifetime
membership starts again whenever they reactivate.

Type your **Current password** and press **Deactivate my account**. Then:

* CalDART turns off automatic renewal and any recurring donation, and emails you
  that automatic renewal is off;
* your membership stops counting. CalDART sets it aside and doesn't send renewal
  reminders;
* you are signed out and taken to the sign-in screen, which says *Your account is
  deactivated. Sign in any time to reactivate it.*

Nothing is deleted. To come back, sign in with the same address and password and
press **Reactivate my account** (see :doc:`sign-in`). A system administrator can't
deactivate their own account. If one tries, the card says *A system administrator
cannot deactivate their own account.*


If something looks wrong
========================

If signing in opens the **About you** step of :doc:`join` instead of your dashboard,
your profile is missing something. The step marks each required field that is still
empty. If an account administrator set up your account for you, signing in doesn't
send you to that step, but some required fields may still be empty: fill them in
here. If a detail you can't change is wrong, such as a membership date, contact the
office. An account administrator can correct it.
