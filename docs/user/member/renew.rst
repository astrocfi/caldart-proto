=====
Renew
=====

The **Renew your membership** screen pays for your next term. A renewal starts the
day after your current term ends, so there is no penalty for renewing early: you
never lose days you have already paid for. Open it from **Renew** in the menu, from
the **Renew** button on your :doc:`dashboard`, or from the link in any renewal
reminder email.


What you see
============

The **Where you stand** card comes first. It shows your membership chip, then
*Expires* and the date with the days to go, or *Expired* and the date it ran out
on, and the plan you hold, such as *Annual membership*.

Renewing is for a member. A friend of CalDART, including somebody who joined as a
member and has not paid yet, has nothing to renew: opening **Renew** takes a friend
to :doc:`become-a-member` instead.

Below it is the checkout, headed **Renew your membership**. It works the same way
as the join wizard's payment step (see :doc:`join`): choose the plan, with Annual
chosen for you, add a contribution if you like, tick **Renew automatically each
year** if you want CalDART to renew you from now on, and pay from one of the tabs.


What happens next
=================

When the payment clears, *Thank you — your membership is renewed.* appears and you
return to your dashboard, which shows the new date straight away. The receipt
arrives by email, subject *CalDART: your receipt for $45.00* with your own amount
(see :doc:`payments`).

* If you renew while you are still current, the new term starts the day after the
  present one ends. Renew on 2027/03/01 with an expiry of 2027/06/30, and your new
  expiry is 2028/06/29.
* If your membership has already lapsed, the new term starts today. You do not pay
  for the gap, and you do not get it back.

An Annual term runs 365 days, counting the day it starts, so a term that starts on
2026/03/01 ends on 2027/02/28.


Life members
============

A life member has nothing to renew, so the same menu entry opens **Contribute to
CalDART**: *As a life member you have nothing to renew. A contribution keeps the
DARTs flying.* The **Where you stand** card carries the **Never expires** chip and
the line *You are a life member. Thank you.*, with no date and no plan.

The form below it, **Make a contribution**, takes a contribution alone. **Make this
a recurring donation** under the amount gives the same amount monthly, quarterly,
or yearly (see :doc:`donate`). When the payment clears, *Thank you for your
contribution.* appears.


Renewal reminders
=================

CalDART emails you five times as your term runs out. Each covers a stretch of the
calendar, so you get every one whichever day you joined: a membership bought three
weeks before it expires still brings the last four.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 35 65

   * - When
     - Subject
   * - 31 to 60 days before your term ends
     - *CalDART: your membership expires in 45 days*, with the real count
   * - 8 to 30 days before
     - *CalDART: your membership expires in 21 days*
   * - 1 to 7 days before
     - *CalDART: your membership expires in 3 days*
   * - The day it ends, or up to 6 days after
     - *CalDART: your membership expires today*, or *CalDART: your membership
       expired 2 days ago*
   * - 30 to 60 days after
     - *CalDART: your membership lapsed 30 days ago*

Every reminder links straight to this screen. You are still current on your end
date itself; the membership reads as expired the next morning.

Each reminder is sent once per term, and renewing stops the rest of the series.
Nobody is sent these reminders while automatic renewal covers their term, and
neither are life members, friends, members who are due to become friends, and
deactivated accounts. Automatic renewal sends its own emails, described on
:doc:`payments`; if it stops, the ordinary reminders resume.

Reminders go to the address you sign in with. An account administrator can see on
the **Reminders** screen every reminder CalDART sent, so "was I ever told?" always
has an answer.


If something looks wrong
========================

If you renewed and your dashboard still shows the old date, reload the page. If it
persists, look at **Recent payments** on your dashboard: a payment marked
**Failed** means no money moved and no term was made, so try again. *Your payment
is still being processed.* means the payment is settling; the membership turns on
by itself when it clears. If you were charged and are still shown as expired,
contact the office with the date and the amount.
