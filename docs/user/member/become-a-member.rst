===============
Become a member
===============

On the **Become a member** screen, a friend of CalDART pays dues and becomes a
member. A friend is a supporter with an account who doesn't pay dues. *Choose a plan and
pay your dues: you are a member of CalDART as soon as the payment goes through.
Nothing changes if you leave this page.*

You reach it from **Make me a member**. A friend finds that button on the membership
card of the :doc:`dashboard`, on the **Your kind of account** card of :doc:`profile`,
and on the wall that covers any members-only page (see :doc:`members-only-content`).
It is open to every friend, including one who chose **Join as a friend instead (no
dues)** while joining (see :doc:`join`). If the office made your account and you
haven't paid yet, you reach it from **Pay dues** on the dashboard's membership card.


What you see
============

Under the page's title is the checkout, in a card. It is the same checkout the join
wizard uses (see :doc:`join`):

#. **Membership**: **Annual** ($45.00, *One year*) or **Life** ($650.00, *One
   payment, membership for life*). The first plan on the list is chosen for you. If
   the site doesn't have a membership plan set up, it shows *No membership plan is
   set up yet. Ask an administrator.* instead of the plans, and you can't pay.
#. **Add a contribution**, if you would like to give more on top of the dues.
#. **Renew automatically each year**, if you want CalDART to renew the membership
   for you (see :doc:`payments`).
#. **How would you like to pay?**, the payment tabs.


What happens next
=================

As soon as the payment goes through, *Thank you — you are a member of CalDART.*
appears, and you go back to your dashboard. Its membership card shows the status
**Current** and the date your membership runs to. Members-only pages are open to
you. The receipt arrives by email, subject *CalDART: your receipt for $45.00* with
your own amount.

From then on, you are a member like any other. CalDART sends renewal reminders as
your term runs out (see :doc:`renew`), and you can become a friend again with
**Make me a friend** on :doc:`profile`.

Anything you gave as a friend stays on :doc:`payments`, with its receipts. If you
have a recurring donation, it carries on.


If something looks wrong
========================

Leaving the screen doesn't change anything. You stay a friend until a payment goes
through. If your card is declined, you aren't charged. If you paid and your
dashboard still says **You are a friend of CalDART**, reload the page. If it still
says that, contact the office with the date and the amount.
