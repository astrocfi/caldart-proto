===============
Become a member
===============

The **Become a member** screen is where a friend of CalDART pays dues and becomes a
member. A friend is a supporter with an account who pays no dues. *Choose a plan and
pay your dues: you are a member of CalDART as soon as the payment goes through.
Nothing changes if you leave this page.*

You reach it from **Make me a member**, which a friend finds on the membership card
of the :doc:`dashboard`, on the **Your kind of account** card of :doc:`profile`, and
on the wall of any members-only page (see :doc:`members-only-content`).


What you see
============

The checkout, headed **Join CalDART**, the same one the join wizard uses (see
:doc:`join`):

#. **Membership**: **Annual** ($45.00, *One year*) or **Life** ($650.00, *One
   payment, membership for life*).
#. **Add a contribution**, if you would like to give more on top of the dues.
#. **Renew automatically each year**, if you want CalDART to renew the membership
   for you (see :doc:`payments`).
#. **How would you like to pay?**, the payment tabs.


What happens next
=================

As soon as the payment goes through, *Thank you — you are a member of CalDART.*
appears and you return to your dashboard, which shows a member's membership card
with the **Current** chip and the date your membership runs to. Members-only pages
open to you. The receipt arrives by email, subject *CalDART: your receipt for
$45.00* with your own amount.

From then on you are a member like any other: CalDART sends renewal reminders as
your term runs out (see :doc:`renew`), and you can become a friend again with
**Make me a friend** on :doc:`profile`.

Anything you gave as a friend stays on :doc:`payments`, with its receipts. A
recurring donation you hold carries on.


If something looks wrong
========================

Leaving the screen changes nothing: you stay a friend until a payment goes through.
A declined card leaves nothing charged. If you paid and your dashboard still says
**You are a friend of CalDART**, reload the page. If it persists, contact the office
with the date and the amount.
