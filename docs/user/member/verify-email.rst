==============
Email verified
==============

The **Email verified** screen is where the link in a verification message takes
you. Following the link proves that the email address is yours.


When CalDART sends a verification message
=========================================

CalDART sends one to an address in three cases:

* when you create an account in :doc:`join`;
* when you move your account to another address on :doc:`change-email`;
* when you reactivate an account whose address was never verified (see
  :doc:`sign-in`).

The message has the subject *CalDART: verify your email address*. Its link is good
for three days.


What you see
============

The screen opens with **Verifying your email address** and *Checking the link…*
while it asks CalDART. It then says **Email verified**, with the address, and a
**Continue** button. It works in any browser, signed in or not, so it does not
matter which device your email program opens it on.

**Continue** takes you on to the right place:

* the join wizard's profile step, if you are part way through joining (see
  :doc:`join`);
* your :doc:`dashboard`, if you are signed in and your profile is complete;
* the **Sign in** screen with your address already filled in, if you are not
  signed in. Signing in there carries you on into the join wizard.


If the link does not work
=========================

The screen says **Email not verified** with the reason, usually *That verification
link is invalid or has expired.* Sign in, and press **Resend verification
message** on your dashboard to get a new one.

An address that is still unverified does not lock you out of anything. Your
dashboard shows a **Verify your email address** card until you follow the link,
and the join wizard waits on its **Check your email** step.


If something looks wrong
========================

If no message arrives, look in your spam or junk folder first, then press **Resend
verification message**. If the address itself is wrong, correct it on
:doc:`change-email`; a fresh message goes to the corrected address. Resending is
limited to a few times an hour. After that the screen says *Request was
throttled. Expected available in ... seconds.*, and you wait that long.
