===============
Change password
===============

The **Change your password** screen replaces a password you still know. *You stay
signed in on this device.* Open it from **Change password** in the menu. If you
have forgotten your password, use :doc:`forgot-password` instead.


What you see
============

Three boxes:

**Current password**
  The password you signed in with.

**New password**
  At least eight characters, and not a password you have used elsewhere.

**Repeat new password**
  The new password again.

Press **Change password**. **Back to the dashboard** leaves without changing
anything.


What happens next
=================

*Your password has been changed.* appears and the boxes empty. You stay signed in
on this device. From then on you sign in with the new password.


If the screen says something else
=================================

*That is not your current password.*
  The first box is wrong. Type it again.

*The two passwords do not match.*
  The second and third boxes differ.

A message under **New password** means the password broke one of CalDART's rules:
too short, too common, all numbers, or too like your name or email address. The
rules are on :doc:`reset-password`.


If something looks wrong
========================

If you cannot remember your current password, sign out and use
:doc:`forgot-password`. Nobody at CalDART can tell you your password or see it.
