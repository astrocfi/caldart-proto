===============
Change password
===============

On the **Change password** screen, you replace a password you still know. *You stay signed in
on this device.* Open it from **Change password** under **My account** in the menu. If you
have forgotten your password, use :doc:`forgot-password` instead.


What you see
============

Three boxes:

**Current password**
  The password you signed in with.

**New password**
  The hint says *At least 8 characters. Avoid common passwords such as password1.*

**Repeat new password**
  The new password again.

Press **Change password**. To leave without changing anything, choose another screen
in the menu.


What happens next
=================

*Your password has been changed.* appears, and the boxes are cleared. You stay
signed in on this device, and every other device signed in to the account is signed
out. From then on, you sign in with the new password.

CalDART emails you *CalDART: your password was changed*, with the date and time. If
you didn't change it, somebody else knows your password. Follow the link in the
email to :doc:`forgot-password` and choose a new password at once.


If the screen says something else
=================================

*That is not your current password.*
  The first box is wrong. Type it again.

*The two passwords do not match.*
  The second and third boxes differ.

A message under **New password** means the password broke one of CalDART's rules:
too short, too common, all numbers, or too much like your name or email address. The
rules are on :doc:`reset-password`.


If something looks wrong
========================

If you can't remember your current password, sign out and use
:doc:`forgot-password`. Nobody at CalDART can tell you your password or see it.
