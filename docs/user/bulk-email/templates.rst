=========
Templates
=========

**Templates** keeps the messages you send again and again, such as reminders about an
exercise, a meeting notice, or a call for volunteers. A new email can then start from one of
them, instead of from a blank page. Every member of CalDART management shares the same
templates. CalDART management finds the screen under **Bulk email** in the menu. A system
administrator can open it too.

A template holds a message, but it doesn't say who to send it to. You choose the people on
:doc:`compose`, or save them as a recipient group on :doc:`groups`.


The list
========

There is one line per template, in order of name:

- **Name**: what the template is called when you choose it. Press it to open the template
  in the form below the heading.
- **Subject**: the subject it starts an email with. A field in it shows as a chip.
- **Type**: the type of email it starts as, such as *Operational*, or a dash if it doesn't
  have one.
- **Last edited**: the day it last changed.
- **Actions**, last: the trashcan, which deletes the template after you press **Delete**.
  Emails already started from it keep their words.

On a narrow screen, **Type**, then **Last edited**, and then **Subject** drop out. On a
phone, the table scrolls sideways, a note above it says so, and **Name** stays pinned at the
left. Until the first template is saved, the table says *No templates yet* and has its own
**New template** button.


Making and changing a template
==============================

**New template** opens an empty form. A template's name opens the form on that template, and
that is also how you rename it. The cursor starts in the form's first box. The Escape key
closes the form, as **Cancel** does, and puts you back on the button or name that opened it.
The form is the compose screen's **What it says** card, plus a name:

- **Name**: required, such as *Monthly newsletter*. Two templates can't share a name, even if
  the capital letters differ.
- **Type of email**: one button for each type, with the sentence saying what it is for, as
  on the compose screen. A draft started from the template takes that type. **No type**
  leaves the choice to each draft.
- **Subject**, **Replies go to**, and **Message**. **Replies go to** is where replies go. As
  on the compose screen, the hint under it names the address used if you leave it empty. The
  message has the same buttons as on :doc:`compose`, and **Insert field** sits beside the
  subject and among the message's buttons. A field such as **First name** shows in the
  message as a chip, as it does there. It stays a field in the template, and it is filled in
  for each person when an email goes.

**Add template** saves a new template, and **Save changes** saves an edited one. A line such
as *Monthly newsletter saved.* confirms it. **Cancel** closes the form without saving. If a
field is refused, the reason shows under it, as on the compose screen.

A quicker way to make a template is **Save as a template** at the top of **What it says** on
the compose screen. It asks for a **Template name** and saves the subject, message, type, and
address for replies you have there. A line such as *Saved as the template Monthly newsletter.
Find it under Templates.* confirms it, and **Templates** in that line links to this screen.


Using a template
================

On :doc:`compose`, **Start from a template** at the top of **What it says** opens a
**Template** drop-down. Choose one and press **Use this template**. The email takes the
template's subject, message, type, and address for replies. If the template leaves the
address blank, the usual address is used. The recipient list stays. On a scheduled email, a
different type takes it back to your drafts, as :doc:`drafts` explains. If you have already
written something, it asks first. It says the template replaces your subject, message, and
address for replies, and your type too if the template has one. It goes ahead only once you
press **Replace my words**.

The email is a copy. Changing it afterward leaves the template as it was, and changing the
template later leaves the emails already started from it as they are.
