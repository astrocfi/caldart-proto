=======================
Bulk email for a DART
=======================

A DART leader can write one email to every member and friend of their own DART (Disaster
Airlift Response Team, one of CalDART's local groups) at once: a training night, a call for
pilots, a change of meeting place, or a mission callout asking who can fly (:doc:`callouts`).
You use the same three screens CalDART management uses, under **Bulk email** in the menu:
**Compose** (:doc:`compose`), **Drafts and scheduled** (:doc:`drafts`), and **Sent**
(:doc:`sent`). This page covers what is different for a DART leader.


Your DART
=========

Your DART is the one named on your own profile, on **My profile**
(:doc:`../member/profile`). Every email you write goes only to people in that DART. If you
change the DART on your profile later, the emails you write from then on go to that DART.

If your profile doesn't name a DART, **Compose** shows *Your profile doesn't name a DART, so
you can't send to anybody. Set your DART on My profile.* instead of the email, in a box with
a link, **Open My profile**. The same box appears at the top of **Drafts and scheduled**,
**Sent**, and **Callouts**. Your **Dashboard** has it too, worded *Bulk email needs a DART.
Set yours on My profile.* None of them offers **New email** until your DART is set. Choose
your DART on your profile and press **Compose** again. An email you started earlier shows a
line instead of the filters. The line says the email belongs to you and your profile doesn't
name a DART, so nobody can be added. CalDART management sees the same line if they open it.


Choosing who gets it
====================

On **Compose**, card **1. Who gets it** says *Sending to the Marin DART* (with your DART's
name) where CalDART management sees a **DART** filter. Every other filter works as it does
for management, inside your DART: **Kind**, **Search**, **Membership**, **Certificate**,
**Medical**, **County**, **Role**, and **Expiring within (days)**. The people they match show
under the filters before you add them, and they are all in your DART. If you don't choose a
filter, that is every member and friend of your DART, you included. **Add these people** adds them.

Somebody can leave your DART after you add them, for example by moving to another DART. They
then show *Not in your DART* under **Will receive?**, and they aren't sent a copy.


The type of email
=================

Card **2. What it says** offers the types of email a DART leader may send. To begin with,
these are Operational and Mission email. A system administrator decides which types each
role may send on **Email types** (:doc:`email-types`). Everything else on the screen, from
the message to **Send** and **Schedule for later**, works as :doc:`compose` describes.


Your drafts and your sent email
===============================

**Drafts and scheduled** and **Sent** list only the emails you wrote. Nobody else's drafts
appear there, and other DART leaders don't see yours. Until you send one, **Sent** says
*Emails you send appear here once they start going out.*, with a **New email** button that
opens Compose. CalDART management sees every bulk email, with who wrote it and the DART it
went to.


If something looks wrong
========================

An email you scheduled may go back to your drafts with *This email was not sent: your
profile doesn't name a DART, so you can't send to anybody. Set your DART on My profile and
send again.* That means your profile lost its DART before the email started. Set your DART
on **My profile**, open the email from **Drafts and scheduled**, check the recipient list,
and send it again. If somebody you expected is missing from the recipient list, check their
DART. You can add only people whose profile names your DART. A member's DART is on their
profile, which an account administrator can change.

An email may also go back to your drafts with *Your DART changed, so this email was not
sent. Add the people again and send when it is ready.* That means your profile names a
different DART from the one you built the recipient list in. Open the email from **Drafts
and scheduled**, press **Remove everyone**, add the people of your DART, and send it again.
If you stop a send and then finish it with **Send the rest**, it skips anybody who has left
your DART since, with *Not in your DART*. It also skips anybody else who can't receive it any
more.
