==========
Bulk email
==========

To send your first bulk email, press **Compose** under **Bulk email** in the menu. In
**1. Who gets it**, choose filters, such as a DART or a county, and press **Add to batch**
until the batch holds everybody the email is for. In **2. What it says**, choose the type
of email, write the subject and the message, and press **Send me a test** to see it in your
own mail program. In **3. Check and send**, fix anything the checks mark **Must fix**, read
the preview, and press **Send to 38 people** (with your own count), which asks you to
confirm. You then have two minutes to cancel, and **Sent** shows what became of each
person's copy once it goes.

These pages cover every screen under **Bulk email**. CalDART management writes to any
member or friend, and a DART leader writes to their own DART (a Disaster Airlift Response
Team, one of CalDART's local groups). The words mean the same on every page:

- The **batch** is the list of people one email goes to, and each of them receives a
  **copy** of their own, with their own details filled in.
- The **type** of an email, such as Operational, Fundraising, or Mission, says what it is
  about. Members and friends can turn off a type they would rather not receive, and a
  system administrator decides which roles send each type.
- A **template** is a message kept to start another email from, and a **recipient group**
  is a set of people kept to add to a batch in one step.
- A **mission callout** is a bulk email asking who can fly, with answer buttons in each
  copy. Each time its non-responders are reminded is another **round** of copies.

Each member and friend reads the bulk email sent to them on :doc:`../member/messages` and
chooses the types they receive on :doc:`../member/email-preferences`, also under **Bulk
Email**.
