==========
Bulk Email
==========

The screens CalDART management and DART leaders use to write one email to many members and
friends at once, under **Bulk Email** in the menu, with the messages and the groups of people
CalDART management keeps to use again, the kinds of bulk email and who may send each, and the
check that tells you whether the email will arrive.
