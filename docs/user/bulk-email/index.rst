==========
Bulk Email
==========

The screens CalDART management uses to write one email to many members and friends at
once, under **Bulk Email** in the menu, with the messages and the groups of people kept to
use again, the kinds of bulk email and who may send each, and the check that tells you
whether the email will arrive.
