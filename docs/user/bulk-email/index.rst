==========
Bulk email
==========

To send your first bulk email, press **Compose** under **Bulk email** in the menu. In
**1. Who gets it**, choose filters, such as a DART or a county, and press **Add these people**.
Repeat until the recipient list has everybody the email is for. In **2. What it says**, choose
the type of email, write the subject and the message, and press **Send me a test** to see it
in your own mail program. In **3. Check and send**, fix anything the checks mark **Must fix**
and read the preview. Then press **Send to 38 people** (with your own count) and confirm. You
have two minutes to cancel. Once the email goes, **Sent** shows what happened to each
person's copy.

These pages cover every screen under **Bulk email**. CalDART management writes to any
member or friend, and a DART leader writes to their own DART (a Disaster Airlift Response
Team, one of CalDART's local groups). These words mean the same thing on every page:

- The **recipient list** is the list of people one email goes to, and each of them receives a
  **copy** of their own, with their own details filled in.
- The **type** of an email, such as Operational, Fundraising, or Mission, says what it is
  about. Members and friends can turn off a type they would rather not receive, and a
  system administrator decides which roles can send each type.
- A **template** is a saved message to start another email from. A **recipient group** is a
  saved set of people to add to a recipient list in one step.
- A **mission callout** is a bulk email asking who can fly, with answer buttons in each
  copy. Each time you remind the people who haven't answered, CalDART sends another
  **round** of copies.

Each member and friend reads the bulk email sent to them on :doc:`../member/messages`, and
chooses the types they receive on :doc:`../member/email-preferences`. Both are under **My
account** in the menu.
