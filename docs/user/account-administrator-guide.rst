===========================
Account administrator guide
===========================

For account administrators: creating and editing members and their profiles,
granting or extending membership terms by hand, maintaining the aircraft and
insurance records, reviewing payment history and the monthly / yearly payment
summary, and running the filtered membership and aircraft reports as CSV or
PDF.  This page will be written on ``feat/docs`` in Phase 3, covering the admin
screens from ``feat/members-admin`` and ``feat/payments``.
