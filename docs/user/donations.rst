=========
Donations
=========

How to give to |org|: from the portal if you are a member or a friend, or from
the public donation page if you are not.  Either way the money reaches the same
books, and a receipt reaches your inbox the moment the payment clears.  What
happens to money after it arrives is in the :doc:`treasurer-guide`.


Giving to CalDART
=================

If you are a member or a friend, sign in and give from the portal:

* **Donate** in the portal menu gives once, or on a schedule — monthly,
  quarterly, or yearly (see :ref:`recurring-donations`).
* Joining and renewing each let you add a contribution to your dues, so one
  payment and one receipt cover both.

Gifts you make from the portal appear on your Payments screen, with their
receipts, and count towards your contribution statement.

The public donation page will not take a gift from your address: it answers
*An account already uses that email address. Sign in to donate.*  Sign in and use
**Donate** instead, so the gift lands on your own record.


.. _public-donation-page:

The public donation page
========================

**Donate** on the public site (``/donate/``) takes a gift from anybody, with no
account and no password.  The page opens with a few words from |org|, then the
form:

#. **Amount.**  Choose one of the tiers, or **Other amount** and type your own in
   whole dollars.  Nothing is refused with *Choose an amount to give.*
#. **About you.**  **First name**, **Last name**, **Email**, and **Phone** are
   required.  The receipt goes to the email address, and the phone number takes
   ten digits (the dashes write themselves).
#. **Tell us more (optional).**  Closed until you open it: your address, city,
   state, ZIP code, and California county; your home airport and its city; your
   DART; your Air Care Alliance number, pilot certificate, and whether you are IFR
   rated; and the volunteer interests you would help with.  Skip it entirely if
   you like.
#. **Continue to payment.**  The form checks the required fields first, then
   shows what you are giving and as whom, for example *You are giving $100.00 as
   Rosa Delgado (rosa@example.org).*  **Change** takes you back to the form with
   everything you typed still there.
#. **Pay.**  Choose how to pay — card (with Apple Pay and Google Pay where your
   browser offers them) or PayPal — exactly as a member does in the portal.  A
   card that asks for an extra check with your bank may take you away from the
   page for a moment; you come back to it, and it confirms the gift.

Once the gift has gone through, the form gives way to |org|'s thanks and one
line: *A receipt is on its way to rosa@example.org.*  A payment that fails leaves
the form where it was, saying why, and nothing is charged.


What a donor gets
=================

Giving from the public page makes you a **donor** (see
:ref:`kinds-of-account`).  The record |org| keeps for you holds your name, your
phone number, whatever you told us in the optional section, and every gift.

* **A receipt for every gift**, emailed with the PDF attached.  It carries the
  sentence *No goods or services were provided in exchange for this
  contribution*, and has no link to the portal, because a donor cannot sign in.
* **One record, however often you give.**  A second gift from the same email
  address, in any mix of capitals, goes on the same record.  The names and phone
  number you give with a completed gift replace the ones stored; anything in the
  optional section that you fill in on a completed gift replaces what you told
  us before, and anything you leave blank keeps it.  Starting, but not
  finishing, a gift changes nothing on the record.
* **No sign-in, no password, no lists.**  A donor has no password and cannot
  ask for one, and appears in no member list, roster, or report of members.  The
  treasurer sees your gifts on the books, and a user administrator can correct a
  mistyped address (see :doc:`user-administrator`).

If you later join as a member or a friend, register with the same email address
and follow the link |org| emails there: your record, and every gift on it,
becomes your portal account.
