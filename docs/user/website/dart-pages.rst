==========
DART pages
==========

Each Disaster Airlift Response Team (a DART, one of the local teams at a general
aviation airport) has a page on the public site, listed in the **DARTs** directory under
**About Us**. This page explains what those pages show and how to keep them right.

What you see
============

The directory
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The **DARTs** page is a DART index. Above its table sit an **Intro** and a **Body** you
write. The table lists every published DART page, alphabetically by title, with the
team's **DART** name, its **Airport**, and its **Leader**. The caption counts the teams.

The home page's **Find your DART** box lists every active team; choosing one and pressing
**Go** opens that team's page, or the directory when the team has no published page.

One team's page
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A DART page has three things you fill in, and one it reads for itself:

* **Dart**, the team this page belongs to, chosen from the teams the membership records
  hold. The list shows each team's name with its airport identifiers.
* **Leader name** and **Leader contact**, under **DART leader**. The contact is an email
  address or a phone number, and visitors can click it to write or call.
* **Body**, the page's own words, built from blocks (see :doc:`pages-and-blocks`).

The airport shown on the page and in the directory comes from the team chosen under
**Dart**, so the identifiers are never typed twice. A **This team** box beside the body
shows the airport and the leader, and a link at the foot returns to **All DARTs**.

What you can do
===============

Add a team's page
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. In **Pages**, open **About Us**, then **DARTs**, and choose **Add child page**. The
   editor opens a DART page straight away, since that is the only type allowed there.
#. Type the team's name as the title. The directory sorts by it.
#. Choose the team under **Dart**, and fill in the leader.
#. Write the body, then choose **Publish**.

A team must exist in the membership records before its page can name it. An account
administrator adds teams in the portal.

Change a leader
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Open the team's page, change **Leader name** and **Leader contact**, and publish again.

If something looks wrong
========================

If a page shows the wrong airport, or none, the airport comes from the team's record in
the portal, which an account administrator corrects; every page that names the team
follows. Check too that **Dart** names the right team. If **Find your DART** opens the
directory for a team, that team has no published page, or its page names a different
team under **Dart**.
