===================
Settings and themes
===================

**Settings**, then **Site settings**, in the editor's left-hand menu holds the details that
appear on every page of the public site, and the theme that decides its colors and
typefaces. This page explains each setting and how to choose a theme.

What you see
============

The settings come in four groups. Press **Save** at the foot of the form to keep your
changes.

**Organization**

* **Org name**, the organization's full name. It ends every page's title in the browser
  tab, heads the footer, and names the member portal. It also begins the subject of the
  emails the site sends, such as receipts.
* **Tagline**, shown under the logo, and used as a page's description for search engines
  when the page has none of its own.
* **EIN**, shown in the footer and on the contact page.

**Contact**

* **Contact email**, shown in the footer, on the contact page, and on the members-only
  page, and given in the emails the site sends as the address for questions.
* **Duty officer phone**, the number to call about a mission. It is shown at the top of
  every page, in the footer, and on the contact page, and it is the only phone number
  the site publishes.
* **Mailing address**, shown at the top of every page, in the footer, and on the contact
  page.

**Links**

* **Donate url**, which adds a **Donate** button to the home page's welcome box when it
  is filled in.
* **Facebook url** and **Twitter url**, shown as **Facebook** and **X / Twitter** links in
  the footer when they are filled in.

**Appearance**

* **Theme**, the look of the whole site (see below).
* **Footer text**, the small print in the footer.

What you can do
===============

Choose a theme
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Theme** offers fourteen looks. Each changes the colors and the typefaces:

* **Duty (default, blue and red)**: a blue-gray ground, a white page, navy menus, and the
  logo's red for the one urgent button.
* **Sierra (warm paper)**: warm paper, deep conifer green, and signal orange.
* **Pacific (cool paper)**: cooler paper and a deep blue.
* **Night (dark)**: a dark palette.
* **Squadron (logo blue on white)**: the logo's cobalt and crimson on white.
* **Flight deck (logo blue, dark)**: the logo's colors on a deep navy panel.
* **Contrail (logo blue, sky paper)**: the logo's colors on pale sky paper.
* **Sectional (aeronautical chart)**: chart cream, chart blue, airspace magenta, and
  terrain tan.
* **Tarmac (concrete and asphalt)**: industrial grays with safety yellow.
* **Coastal (fog and ocean teal)**: fog paper, ocean teal, sunset coral, and dune sand.
* **Slate (cool corporate)**: slate blue-gray with amber.
* **Meridian (high-contrast civic)**: white, navy, and burnt orange, with one typeface
  throughout.
* **Monterey night (charcoal dark)**: charcoal with a sea-green and amber.
* **Granite (near-monochrome)**: white, near-black, and grays, with one blue for links.

Every theme meets the same standard for contrast, so no choice makes the site harder to
read. Saving a theme changes the public site and the member portal at once, so the two
always match.

Preview a theme first
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

While you are signed in as a website or system administrator, add ?theme= and the
theme's first word in lowercase to the end of any page's address, such as
caldart.org/about/?theme=night. For the two-word names use a hyphen:
?theme=flight-deck or ?theme=monterey-night. The preview lasts only for the page you
added it to, and it changes nothing for anybody else.

If something looks wrong
========================

If a detail in the footer or at the top of the page is wrong, it comes from these
settings, so correct it here and save; every page follows. If a footer link is missing,
its setting is empty. If a theme looks wrong for one person only, they may be previewing
another theme with ?theme= in the address.
