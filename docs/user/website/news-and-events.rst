===============
News and events
===============

The **News** page lists the network's news posts, and the **Events** page is its calendar
of exercises, workshops, drills, and meetings. This page explains how to post news, how
to put an event on the calendar, and where each shows up on the site.

What you see
============

News
~~~~

The **News** page is a news index: it lists the posts beneath it, newest first, eight to
a page. The home page lists the three most recent public posts by itself, with a link to
**All news**, so there is nothing to update by hand when you publish one.

A news post has a **Post date**, an **Intro** shown in the listings, an optional
**Image**, and a **Body** built from the same blocks as any other page (see
:doc:`pages-and-blocks`). Its **Access** section has the **Members only** switch (see
:doc:`members-only`). A members-only post is left out of the news list for visitors who
could not open it, and never appears on the home page.

Events
~~~~~~

The **Events** page is the calendar. It lists every event still ahead under **Coming
up**, soonest first, and then what has already happened under **Already happened**, most
recent first, twenty to a page. The home page's **Upcoming events** box shows the three
soonest events, with a link to **All events**, and the box disappears when nothing is
coming up.

An event has an **Event date**, a **Time** (for example, *9 am to 1 pm*), a
**Location** (the airport and town, or who it is for), an **Intro** of one line shown in
the calendar, and a **Body** for the details. Time and location are both optional; the
site shows them together on one line, such as *Reid-Hillview (KRHV), 9 am to 1 pm*.
Events are always public.

What you can do
===============

Post news
~~~~~~~~~

#. In **Pages**, open **News** and choose **Add child page**. The editor opens a news
   post straight away, since that is the only type allowed there.
#. Type the title and set the **Post date**.
#. Write the **Intro**, choose an **Image** if you have one, and build the **Body**.
#. Turn on **Members only** if the post is for current members alone.
#. **Preview**, then choose **Publish**.

Put an event on the calendar
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

#. In **Pages**, open **Events** and choose **Add child page**.
#. Type the title. It is what people see in the sidebar and on the calendar.
#. Set the **Event date**. That date alone decides where the event appears: today or
   later puts it under **Coming up** and in the home page's sidebar, and yesterday or
   earlier moves it to **Already happened**. Nothing needs tidying away afterwards.
#. Fill in **Time** and **Location** if they help.
#. Write the one-line **Intro** and the **Body**.
#. Choose **Publish**. A draft is invisible until you do.

Correct or take down a post or an event
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

To correct one, open it, change it, and publish again. To take one down, choose
**Unpublish**. Deleting is worth it only for something posted by mistake, since a past
event is a record of what the network did.

If something looks wrong
========================

If a new post is missing from the home page, check that it is published, that it is not
members only, and that its **Post date** is recent enough to be among the three newest.
If an event is missing from the sidebar, check its **Event date** and that it is
published; the sidebar shows only the three soonest. If **Add child page** does not
offer a news post or an event, you are under the wrong parent: posts go under **News**
and events under **Events**.
