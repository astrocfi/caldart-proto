=====================
The members-only area
=====================

Some pages and files are for current members alone: notices, handbooks, bylaws, minutes,
and member forms. This page explains how to close a page or a file to everyone else, and
who gets past the closure.

What you see
============

Who can read a members-only page or file
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* a signed-in member whose membership is current, and
* anyone signed in with a role beyond plain member: a DART leader, the treasurer, or an
  account, user, website, or system administrator.

Everybody else gets a page with the title and one clear next step in place of the
content. A visitor who is not signed in is asked to sign in or join. A member whose
membership has lapsed is asked to renew. A friend of the network (someone with an account
who is not a member) is asked to become a member. Anybody else signed in is asked to
join. Search engines never see the content.

The site starts with a **Members Only** page in the top menu, with **Notices** and
**Documents and Links** beneath it, all three closed.

What you can do
===============

Close a page
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Standard pages and news posts have a **Members only** switch in their **Access** section.
Turn it on and publish, and the page is closed. The switch belongs to one page: turning
it on for **Members Only** does not close the pages beneath it, so turn it on for each
page you want closed. A closed page in the top menu moves to the right-hand end, beside
**Sign in**.

A closed news post is left out of the news list for anyone who could not open it, and
never appears on the home page.

Close a file
~~~~~~~~~~~~

A file is closed by the collection it lives in. When you upload a document, choose the
**Members only** collection, and it is served to exactly the people who can read a
members-only page. Everyone else gets the same page asking them to sign in, renew, or
join, whether they followed a link or typed the address.

Collections can sit inside one another, and anything in a collection beneath **Members
only** is closed too. A file in any other collection, including **Root**, where a file
lands unless you choose otherwise, is open to anyone with the link. Put handbooks,
rosters (each DART's monthly list of its members), bylaws, and member forms in **Members
only**, and leave flyers, logos, and public forms outside it.

To move a file that is already uploaded, open it under **Documents**, change its
**Collection**, and save; links to it keep working. The **Documents** list has a
**Collection** filter for checking what sits in **Members only**. When you upload several
files at once, the collection you pick applies to all of them, so upload members-only
files separately from public ones.

Wagtail's own privacy setting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A page's **Status** panel also offers **Change privacy**, the editor's own setting, which
can close a page with a password or to certain editor groups, and which applies to every
page beneath it. Use the **Members only** switch for "current members only", because it
knows when a membership lapses.

If something looks wrong
========================

If a members-only page shows you the sign-in page too, you are signed out of the member
portal; sign in there, and any role beyond plain member lets you through. If a member
says a page is closed to them, their membership may have lapsed, and the page tells them
so and offers **Renew my membership**. If a file you meant to close opens for anyone,
check its **Collection** under **Documents**.
