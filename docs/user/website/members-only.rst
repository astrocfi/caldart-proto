=====================
The members-only area
=====================

Some pages and files are for current members alone: notices, handbooks, bylaws, minutes,
and member forms. This page explains how to close a page or a file to everyone else, and
who can still read it.

What you see
============

Who can read a members-only page or file
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* a signed-in member whose membership is current, and
* anyone signed in with a role beyond plain member, such as a DART leader, the
  treasurer, or an administrator.

Everybody else sees the page's title and one clear next step instead of the content. A
visitor who isn't signed in is asked to sign in or join. A member whose membership has
lapsed is asked to renew. A friend of the network (someone with an account who isn't a
member) is asked to become a member. Anybody else signed in is asked to pay dues. Search engines never see the content.

The site starts with a **Members Only** page in the top menu, with **Notices** and
**Documents and Links** beneath it, all three closed.

What you can do
===============

Close a page
~~~~~~~~~~~~

Standard pages and news posts have a **Members only** switch in their **Access** section.
Turn it on and publish, and the page is closed. The switch covers only its own page.
Turning it on for **Members Only** doesn't close the pages beneath it, so turn it on for
each page you want closed. A closed page in the top menu moves to the right-hand end, beside
**Sign in**.

Anyone who can't open a closed news post doesn't see it in the news list, and it never
appears on the home page.

Close a file
~~~~~~~~~~~~

A file's collection decides whether it is closed. When you upload a document, choose the
**Members only** collection. Then only the people who can read a members-only page can
open it. Everyone else gets the same page asking them to sign in, renew, or
join, whether they followed a link or typed the address.

Collections can sit inside one another, and anything in a collection beneath **Members
only** is closed too. A file in any other collection is open to anyone with the link.
That includes **Root**, where a file goes unless you choose otherwise. Put handbooks,
rosters (each DART's monthly list of its members), bylaws, and member forms in **Members
only**, and leave flyers, logos, and public forms outside it.

To move a file that is already uploaded, open it under **Documents**, change its
**Collection**, and save. Links to it keep working. The **Documents** list has a
**Collection** filter to check what is in **Members only**. When you upload several
files at once, the collection you pick applies to all of them, so upload members-only
files separately from public ones.

Wagtail's own privacy setting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

A page's **Status** panel also offers **Change privacy**, the editor's own setting. It
can close a page with a password, or open it only to certain editor groups, and it applies
to every page beneath it. Use the **Members only** switch for "current members only", because it
knows when a membership lapses.

If something looks wrong
========================

If a members-only page asks you to sign in too, you are signed out of the member portal.
Sign in there, and any role beyond plain member lets you through. If a member
says a page is closed to them, their membership may have lapsed, and the page tells them
so and offers **Renew my membership**. If a file you meant to close opens for anyone,
check its **Collection** under **Documents**.
