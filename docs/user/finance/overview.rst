==================
The money overview
==================

The **Payments** screen under **Administration** answers "how are we doing?" at a glance:
what came in, what the payment providers kept, what reached the bank, and what went back
in refunds. It is the first tab of the finance area, and the one the menu opens.

Who sees this screen
====================

The treasurer and an account administrator. A treasurer sees every payment, fee, refund,
and automatic renewal, and never the medical and certificate details on a member's
record. An account administrator sees the same money and the member records as well.

Moving around the finance area
==============================

A row of tabs sits at the top of every finance screen:

* **Overview**, this screen.
* **Payments**, every payment, one row each (see :doc:`payment-list`).
* **Renewals**, the automatic renewals and recurring donations people have set up (see
  :doc:`renewals`).
* **Reconciliation**, the totals laid out for matching against a bank statement (see
  :doc:`reconciliation`).
* **Contributions**, everyone who gave in one calendar year (see :doc:`contributions`).
* **Donors**, everyone who has given through the public donation page (see
  :doc:`donors`). Only the treasurer has this tab.

When you open one payment, one member's money history, or the form for recording a
check, the tabs stay in place with **Payments** marked, so you can always see where you
are.

Every table in the finance area fills the width of your browser window. A table wider
than the window scrolls sideways inside its own frame.

What you see
============

The three figures across the top
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Three tiles, each covering every payment on the site:

* **This month**, the calendar month you are in.
* **Year to date**, from January 1 to today.
* **Last 12 months**, a rolling twelve-month window.

Each tile shows the gross taken in large type, how many payments made it up, and
beneath that the **Fees** the providers kept, the **Net** that reached the bank, and
what was **Refunded**. A card that was declined never brought in any money, so it is
left out. A payment that was later refunded did bring money in, so it is counted, with
the refund shown beside it.

Payments by period
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Below the tiles, **Payments by period** adds the same money up by month or by year. The
**Month** and **Year** buttons switch between the two. The newest period is at the top.

For each period the table shows the number of **Payments**, how much was **Dues**, how
much was **Contributions**, a column for each payment provider that took money in the
range, the **Fees**, the **Net**, what was **Refunded**, and the **Total**.

To compare this year with last year, choose **Year** and read down the **Total**
column.

What you can do
===============

Narrow the table
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Above the period table, **From**, **To**, **Provider**, **Status**, and **Search** narrow
what the table adds up. They work exactly as they do on the payment list (see
:doc:`payment-list`). The three tiles ignore them: **This month** is always this month.

Each filter applies as soon as you set it. A typed one applies once you pause. **Reset to
Defaults** clears them all. The filters are kept in the page's address, so you can
bookmark a filtered view or send the link to another treasurer.

Go to the list
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**All payments**, at the top right, opens the payment list.

Reports by email
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The **Reports** screen, also under **Administration**, sends the payments,
reconciliation, and contributions reports, and for the treasurer the donors report, to you
or to anyone else allowed to read them, on a schedule you choose.

If something looks wrong
========================

If the tiles do not match a provider's own dashboard, check three ordinary reasons
first. The tiles count only money that arrived, and a provider's dashboard may count
attempts too. The tiles show the gross, and a dashboard often shows the net. The tiles
date a payment by the day it completed, which can fall a day either side of the
provider's settlement date. The :doc:`reconciliation` screen shows the gross, the fees,
and the net side by side for exactly this comparison. If the period table says *No
payments in this range*, widen the dates or press **Reset to Defaults**.
