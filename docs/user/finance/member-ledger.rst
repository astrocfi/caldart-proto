========================
A member's money history
========================

A member's money history gathers everything one person has paid, given, and been
refunded, their automatic renewal or recurring donation, and the contribution statements
they can be sent. It answers a question about one member's money without searching the
payment list. You reach it by clicking the member's name on a payment's own screen, or
the link **Everything** and the member's name **has paid** at the foot of that screen.

An account administrator sees the same cards on the **Payments** tab of the member's
record. A treasurer, who does not open member records, uses this screen.

What you see
============

The heading is the member's name, with their email address beneath it and a chip on the
right showing where their membership stands.

Totals
~~~~~~

The **Totals** card covers the member's whole history: what they have **Paid**, what they
have **Contributed**, what the providers kept in **Fees**, and what has been
**Refunded**.

Automatic renewal
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The next card describes the member's standing authority to charge them, if they have
one. Its title says what it charges for: **Automatic renewal**, **Recurring donation**,
or **Automatic renewal and contribution**. It shows:

* **State**: **On**, **Waiting for the first payment**, **Paused after failed charges**,
  or **Turned off**.
* **Method**, the saved card or PayPal account.
* **Charges**, the plan (or **Contribution** for a recurring donation) and the amount.
* **Next charge**, the day it falls due.
* **Last refusal**, when the last charge was declined, with the reason the provider gave.
  It is the same wording the member was emailed.

A member with no standing authority sees *This member renews by hand.*

Payments
~~~~~~~~

The **Payments** card lists every payment, including attempts that failed, with the day
it was **Paid**, the **Receipt** number (which opens the payment's own screen, see
:doc:`payment-record`), what it was **For**, the **Total**, what was **Refunded**, the
provider under **Method**, and the **Status**. A member with none sees *No payments
recorded*; a term an administrator granted by hand has no payment behind it.

Contribution statements
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The **Contribution statements** card has a button for every calendar year in which the
member gave something beyond their dues. Each downloads that year's statement as a PDF,
the same document the member can download from their own **Payments** screen. A member
who never gave beyond their dues sees *This member has not given anything beyond their
dues.*

What you can do
===============

Open any payment from its receipt number, and download any year's contribution statement.
To turn a member's automatic renewal or recurring donation off on their behalf, use the
**Renewals** tab (see :doc:`renewals`).

If something looks wrong
========================

If the screen says *That ledger could not be loaded*, the member may have been removed,
or the link may be out of date; find the member again from one of their payments. If a
renewal reads **Paused after failed charges**, the **Last refusal** line says why, and the
member can put a new card on file from their own **Payments** screen.
