================
Record a payment
================

**Record a payment** is for money that never passed through a card or PayPal: a check in
the mail, cash handed over at a meeting, or a bank transfer. Recording it here puts it in
the books, starts whatever membership term it paid for, and emails the member the same
receipt a card payment earns. You reach it from **Record a payment** at the top of the
payment list.

What you see
============

One form, with these fields:

* **Member**, a search box. Type part of a name or an email address and up to ten
  matching people appear beneath it, each with a chip showing their membership. A friend
  (someone with an account who supports CalDART without being a member) or a donor can
  be found the same way. Click the right person to choose them;
  **Choose somebody else** clears the choice.
* **Plan**, the membership plan the money pays for. Choose **No membership** for a
  contribution on its own.
* **Contribution**, the gift in dollars on top of any dues. It starts at nothing.
* **Method**: **Check**, **Cash**, **Bank transfer**, or **Other**.
* **Reference**, the check number, or the bank transfer's own reference.
* **Received on**, the day the money arrived. It starts at today.
* **Note**, anything worth keeping with the payment.

What you can do
===============

Fill in the form and press **Record the payment**. The site confirms with *Recorded* and
the new receipt number, and opens the payment's own screen (see :doc:`payment-record`).

What happens next
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* The payment is recorded as already paid, with **By hand** as its provider and no fee,
  so its net is the whole amount.
* If it paid for a plan, the membership term starts at once.
* The member is emailed their receipt, with the subject *The California DART Network:
  your receipt for* and the amount.
* **Received on** is the date the payment carries everywhere in the books: in the
  headline figures, the period table, reconciliation, and the year-end contributions
  list. Set it to the day the check arrived, which may be earlier than the day you enter
  it.
* The reference is kept as the payment's **Reference**, so a search on the payment list
  for the check number finds it.

Mistakes the form catches
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

* No member chosen: *Choose the member this payment is for.*
* A date after today: *The money cannot have arrived in the future.*
* A reference another recorded payment already carries: *Another recorded payment
  already carries the reference* followed by the number. This stops the same check being
  entered twice.
* A plan for a life member, who has nothing left to renew: *You are a life member, so
  there is nothing to renew. Make a contribution instead.*

If something looks wrong
========================

If the member you want does not appear, try their email address, or part of their last
name. If a check was recorded against the wrong person or for the wrong amount, refund it
from the payment's own screen (see :doc:`payment-record`) and record it again correctly:
a refund of a payment recorded by hand moves no money, so the books simply show the
correction.
