=============
Contributions
=============

The **Contributions** tab lists everyone who gave something beyond their dues in one
calendar year, with what each of them gave. It is the list behind the year-end
acknowledgment letters, and it downloads each giver's contribution statement.

What you see
============

There is one row for each person who made at least one payment with a contribution that
year, largest giver first. Members, friends (people with an account who support CalDART
without being members), and donors are all listed. Each row shows the **Member**, their
**Email**, how many **Payments** carried a contribution, the amount **Given**, what was
**Refunded** against those payments, and the **Net**. The name opens everything the person
has paid (see :doc:`member-ledger`), and the email address opens a new message to them. The
net is the figure an acknowledgment letter quotes: what was given, less anything refunded
against it.

On a narrower screen, **Email**, **Payments**, **Refunded**, and **Given** drop out, in that
order, so the **Net** and the **Statement** button stay beside the name. On a phone, the
table scrolls sideways inside the page, a note above it says so, and the name stays pinned
at the left.

A payment counts in the year its money arrived, so a check received in December counts
in December's year even if you recorded it in January.

What you can do
===============

Choose the year
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Year** shows this year until you choose one of the nine years before it.

Download a statement
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Each row's **Statement** button, in the **Download** column, downloads that person's
contribution statement for the year on screen as a PDF. It is the same document the person
can download from their own **My payments** screen.

CalDART also emails each active account that gave that year its statement, once, with the
subject *CalDART: your 2025 contribution statement* (with that year's number). A system
administrator runs and checks that mailing from the :doc:`../admin/scheduled` screen.

Download the list
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

**Export CSV** gives a file for a mail merge, and **Export PDF** a copy for the board.
Each file's name has the date it was made. Click a column heading to sort the table by it,
and click it again to reverse the order. The table opens sorted by **Net**, largest first,
and every heading except **Statement** sorts. The **Emailed reports** screen can also send
this list on a schedule, for **This year** or **Last year**.

If something looks wrong
========================

If the table says *No contributions that year*, choose another year. Or check on the
payment list that the gifts were recorded with a contribution amount. A payment that was
only dues doesn't appear here. If a person's net looks low, click their name to open their
payment history and see which payment was refunded.
