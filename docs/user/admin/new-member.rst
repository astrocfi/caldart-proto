==========
New member
==========

**New member** creates an account for somebody and fills in as much of their profile as you
know. Use it for a person who joins on paper, at an event, or by phone.

Only an account administrator can open it, from the **New member** button at the top of
:doc:`members`. A system administrator can open it too.


What you see
============

One form in four parts. Only the email address and the first and last names are required;
the phone number never is. Everything else can be filled in later, by you or by the
member. Left empty, a name reads *Enter a first name.* or *Enter a last name.* when you
press **Add member**.

**Account**
   **Email address**, **First name**, **Last name**, **Kind of account**, and
   **Password**. **Kind of account** is **Member**, the first choice, or **Friend**; its
   hint reads *A friend pays no dues and is never current or expired.*

**Contact**, **Amateur radio**, **Aviation**, and **Volunteer interests**
   The same fields a member fills in on their own profile: address and phones, the
   amateur radio callsign, DART, certificate, medical, ratings, hours, and the ways they
   would like to help. None of them
   is required here.

**Administration**
   **How they heard about CalDART** and **Administrator notes**. Only account
   administrators can read these. Members never see them, on any screen.


Creating the account
====================

#. Type the email address, and the name if you have it.
#. Choose **Member** or **Friend**.
#. Leave **Password** empty unless you have a particular reason to set one.
#. Fill in whatever else you know.
#. Press **Add member**. **Cancel** or **Back to members** leaves without saving. If the
   site refuses something, such as an address another account holds, you are taken to the
   first field it refused, however far up the form, and a line beside the button says how
   many fields to check.

The member's record opens, with a message at the top of the screen.


What happens next
=================

**With the password left empty**, the message reads that the person *has been emailed a
link to set a password*. The email's subject is *CalDART: set your password*, with your
organization's name in place of CalDART. Using the link lets them choose a password and also
confirms their email address, since only the owner of the address could have received it.

**With a password typed**, the message reads that the person *can sign in now*. You have to
tell them the password yourself. They are sent an email with the subject *CalDART: verify
your email address*, and their address shows **Unverified** on their record until they open
the link in it.

Either way the person's first sign-in opens the join wizard (see :doc:`../member/join`),
and the rest of the portal stays closed until they finish it:
they verify the address if it is not verified yet, fill in any required profile field you
left empty, and, when the kind is member and no term has started, pay their dues or choose
to be a friend instead. Grant the term first, as below, and complete the profile, and they
go straight to the dashboard.
Creating an account does not give anybody a membership: the account is a friend of CalDART
until a term starts, and reads **Friend** on :doc:`members` and on its record whichever
kind you chose. Grant a term on the **Memberships** tab of the :doc:`member-record`, or let
the member pay online, and they are a member from that moment. A friend needs neither,
because a friend pays no dues.


If something looks wrong
========================

*An account with that email address already exists.* means somebody already has an account
on that address, very often the person in front of you from an earlier membership; capitals
are ignored, so Marta@ and marta@ are the same address. Search for it on :doc:`members` and
edit that record. If the new member says the set-your-password email never came, check the
spelling of the address on their record, then ask a user administrator to send a password
reset from the :doc:`user-record`, which does the same job. If you typed a password, no such
email was sent, and you need to tell them the password.
