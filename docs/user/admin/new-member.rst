==========
New member
==========

**New member** creates an account for somebody and fills in as much of their profile as you
know. Use it for a person who joins on paper, at an event, or by phone.

Only an account administrator can open it, from the **New member** button at the top of
:doc:`members`. A system administrator can open it too.


What you see
============

The form has four parts. Only the email address and the first and last names are required,
and the phone number never is. You or the member can fill in everything else later. If you
leave a required box empty and press **Add member**, the address shows *Enter their email
address.*, and a name shows *Enter a first name.* or *Enter a last name.*

**Account**
   **Email address**, **First name**, **Last name**, and **Kind of account**. **Kind of
   account** is **Member**, the first choice, or **Friend**. Its hint says *A friend doesn't
   pay dues and is never current or expired.* There isn't a password box. The person chooses
   their own password from the email the site sends them, so nobody else ever knows it.

**Contact**, **Amateur radio**, **Aviation**, and **Volunteer interests**
   The same fields a member fills in on their own profile: address and phones, the amateur
   radio callsign, DART, certificate, medical, ratings, hours, and the ways they would like
   to help. None of them is required here.

**Administration**
   **How they heard about CalDART** and **Administrator notes**. Only account
   administrators can see these. Members never see them, on any screen.


Creating the account
====================

#. Type the email address, and the name if you have it.
#. Choose **Member** or **Friend**.
#. Fill in whatever else you know.
#. Press **Add member**. **Cancel** or **Back to members** leaves without saving. If the
   site refuses something, such as an address another account already uses, it takes you
   to the first field it refused, however far up the form it is. A line beside the button
   says how many fields to check.

The member's record opens, with a message at the top of the screen.


What happens next
=================

The message says the person *has been emailed a link to set a password*. The email's
subject is *CalDART: set your password*, with your organization's own name instead of
CalDART. The link lets them choose a password. It also confirms their email address, since
only the owner of the address could have received it. Until they use it, their address
shows **Unverified** on their record.

The person has already joined. They can't sign in until they set a password through the
invitation. After that, their first sign-in opens the dashboard. It never opens the rest of
the join wizard (see :doc:`../member/join`), even if the profile is half empty or there
isn't a term yet. Creating an account doesn't give anybody a membership, though. Until a
term starts, the account shows **Not yet paid** on :doc:`members`, with **Member** as its
kind, and *Not yet paid: grant a term on Memberships* on its record. The member's own
dashboard says *Your membership is not yet paid* and offers **Pay dues**. Grant a term on
the **Memberships** tab of the :doc:`member-record`, or let the member pay online, and they
are a member from that moment. A friend doesn't need either, because a friend doesn't pay
dues.


If something looks wrong
========================

*An account with that email address already exists.* means somebody already has an account
with that address. Very often it is the person in front of you, from an earlier membership.
Capitals don't matter, so Marta@ and marta@ are the same address. Search for it on
:doc:`members` and edit that record. If the new member says the set-your-password email
never came, check the spelling of the address on their record. Then ask a user
administrator to send a password reset from the :doc:`user-record`, which does the same
job.
