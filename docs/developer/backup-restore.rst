==================
Backup and restore
==================

Looking after the data: what ``db_backup``, ``db_restore``, ``db_reset`` and
``health`` do, where dumps are written and how they are rotated, taking and
downloading a backup from the system screen or the API, restoring onto a fresh
database, and the safety rules around resetting an environment.  This page is
owned by ``feat/ops``, which delivers the ``sysadmin`` commands and API.
